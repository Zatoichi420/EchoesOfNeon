# Echoes of Neon: Art Team Notes

Requests for the art team. Status of each: REQUESTED unless marked.

## 1. 3D-printable STL of the House of Corvus crest (REQUESTED 2026-10-08, by Orlando)

Goal: a physical, printable version of the crest. It doubles as a TOUCH PROTOTYPE, because the game reads the crest by touch and the creator is blind: every element must be readable by fingertip, in the order Marcus meets them. Crest design is CANON, see [[Story Canon Log]] (section "The House crest").

Elements to model (relief, one body, no supports if possible):
- Black shield outline, plain and smooth. Silent field.
- Gold closed book with a clasp and a keyhole (no lock), the crow standing on it.
- Crow, wings half open, head turned back over its shoulder, one eye covered by a brass lid (a small raised disc that clicks when pressed if the print allows a separate part).
- Wing notches: THREE shallow, ONE deep, so a fingertip dragged across reads quick, quick, quick, long. This is the spoon tap in metal and is the key tactile discovery.
- Studs across the top: three small, one larger below (also the tap).
- The EMPTY RING at the heart of the shield: open, worn smooth, deliberately blank. Do not fill it and do not put any symbol in it.
- Bell without a clapper above the shield; two crow supporters (one facing, one turned away); scroll with the motto "QUOD MEMINIMUS, TENEMUS" in raised letters with a scraped nine-letter gap (CASSANDRA) in a separate optional version.
- Optional underside: beads in groups 12, 4, 7 and 8 (the date code 12/04/78).

Variants wanted: (a) 90 to 120 mm wall medallion for touch testing; (b) signet ring face (crow's head, worn smooth, clicks against glass); (c) staff badge disc (small enamel-style, with three clicks and one chime if a hardware version is ever made); (d) simplified Aegis-Corvus logo (shield silhouette with the crow's head in negative space and four rim rivets: three small, one large).

Print guidance: raised relief at least 1.2 mm so fingertips read it; keep stud and notch spacing generous; test the wing notches by touch before locking; multi-color via filament swaps (black shield, gold book and bell, white studs). The Flashforge AD5X is the home printer (see global CLAUDE.md for network cautions; do not assume the printer is reachable from the work Mac).

## 2. Marcus's cane is a collapsing short katana (REQUESTED 2026-10-08, by Orlando)
Canon change: the cane COLLAPSES (nested segments fold and lock with a ratchet and a snap) into a SHORT KATANA, straight and light, and folds back into a cane. Concept art for Marcus currently shows a glowing cane (cyan and violet seams); please design the collapsed cane, the mid-transform and the short katana, keeping the cyan and blue glow rule for anything cybernetic. Sound design note: ratchet of nested segments, a snap and lock, a low singing note when the blade moves, a ring of steel on steel. The blinding flash and the sonic emitter stay as separate upgrades.
