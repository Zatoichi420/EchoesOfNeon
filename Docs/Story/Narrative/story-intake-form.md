# Echoes of Neon — Story Intake Form

**Answered 2026-09-13** (`Echoes_of_Neon_Story_Intake.docx`, plus a follow-up
conversation confirming the ending's familial revelation, supernatural twist, and
Max's secret). Full drafted content now lives in `Docs/story-bible.md` and
`Docs/Story/Characters/`. This original questionnaire is kept as-is for reference —
the questions below are what prompted those answers.

This is a fill-in questionnaire, not a finished story bible. It exists so the actual
creative direction comes from Orlando, not from an AI extrapolating a full plot from a
one-paragraph premise. Answer as much or as little as you want, in any order — nothing
here is locked in until you say so.

## What's already established (from the project's own lore bible in `MEMORY.md`)

- **Title:** Echoes of Neon
- **Genre:** Single-player tactical/methodical cyber-noir FPS
- **Setting:** New Carthage — a rain-slicked, neon-lit industrial metropolis run by
  monolithic mega-corporations
- **Protagonist:** Marcus "Echo" Cross — former military breach specialist turned
  industrial contractor
- **Inciting incident:** Aegis-Corvus Dynamics (a mega-corp) murdered Marcus's family,
  stole his late wife's neural memory core, and destroyed his biological eyes to cover
  up illegal bioweapons experiments
- **Marcus's ability:** rebuilt with an underground prototype cybernetic ocular suite
  (the Oculus Sensory Suite) — sonar echolocation, Neural Dilate slow-mo focus,
  predictive ballistics
- **Core design philosophy:** accessibility is woven into the in-lore cyberware and
  HUD, not a bolted-on menu toggle

That's a premise and a protagonist. It is **not yet** a theme, an ending, a three-act
structure, a named antagonist, or a single scene beyond one graybox test range. The
questions below are what's missing.

## Open questions

### Scene list
1. Roughly how many scenes/missions should the full game have?
2. What happens in the opening scene — where does it start, what's Marcus doing right
   before the story kicks off?
3. What are the major story beats in between (the ones you already have in mind, even
   loosely)?
4. What actually happens at the end? Does Marcus get revenge, get answers about his
   wife's memory core, both, neither, something else?
5. Is there a genuine wrong-turn or point of no return anywhere (a choice that fails,
   like a real defeat state), or is the story fully linear?

### Locations
1. What locations in New Carthage does the story visit? (a specific corp tower, a
   slum district, a bar, the wife's old lab, wherever Marcus's hideout is...)
2. For each one you can name: what does it look/feel/sound like? (this doubles as
   input for the Audio intake form's ambient-bed questions)

### Theme and tone
1. What's the one idea you want the ending to pay off — revenge is hollow, justice vs.
   vengeance, memory and identity, something else entirely?
2. What is this story explicitly *not* — tone/genre guardrails? (e.g. not a redemption
   arc, not a found-family story, stays grounded rather than becoming cyberpunk-mystic)

### Characters and costumes
1. Does Aegis-Corvus Dynamics get a named, individual antagonist (a face to hate), or
   does it deliberately stay a faceless corporate threat?
2. Any allies or supporting cast at all — a handler, an informant, a fellow victim of
   the same corp?
3. Marcus's current physical description/costume, post-implant — how visibly
   cybernetic is he, what does he actually wear on a job?
4. For any other named character: physical description/costume?
