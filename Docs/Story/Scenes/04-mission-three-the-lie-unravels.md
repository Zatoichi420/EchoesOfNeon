# Mission 3 (draft) — The Lie Unravels

Built from Orlando's answers (2026-09-16): Mission 3 is a **partial** red herring —
Max sends Marcus after something that isn't fully what it seems, but during the
mission Marcus actually figures out that Max has been lying to him, and this
discovery strengthens his resolve rather than breaking him. **Everything below the
"Confirmed" line in each section is Claude's proposed structure, not stated fact —
correct freely**, same convention as the two mission drafts before this one.

## Confirmed
- Mission 3 follows a lead Max gave Marcus — part of the stalling/deflecting
  pattern established at the end of Mission 2.
- The lead is a **partial** red herring, not a total fabrication — something about
  it is real, something about it is misdirection. Exact split not yet specified.
- **During the mission, Marcus figures out Max has been lying to him the whole
  time.** This is a real discovery Marcus makes himself, not something told to
  him.
- The discovery **strengthens Marcus's resolve** — it doesn't break him or make
  him give up on the people around him, it hardens him further.

## Confirmed answers (Orlando, 2026-09-16)

1. **What's "partial" about the red herring**: Max is giving out **half-truths**
   — not a total fabrication. Matches the proposed reading: something about the
   objective is genuinely real, the deception is in the framing/reasoning around
   it.
2. **Surface objective**: **a decoy Max planted himself** (the earlier draft's
   Option C) — Marcus is sent to retrieve/investigate something physical that
   turns out to be something Max set up in advance, not just a bad lead he passed
   along. This confirms Max is *actively* engineering the deception, not merely
   misdirecting with secondhand bad information.
3. **What tips Marcus off**: **a speech tell.** Max says a particular phrase
   differently than he has for as long as Marcus has known him — a specific,
   personal, audio-only detail Marcus catches because of how attuned his hearing
   is (thematically perfect for an augmented-blind protagonist whose whole life
   runs on sound). The wrongness of *how* it's said, not just what's said, is
   what reveals to Marcus that the moment was **planned/rehearsed** in advance —
   this is what actually proves premeditation, not just a lie of omission.

**New open item this raises**: the actual phrase itself needs writing — something
that recurs naturally across Max's earlier workshop-hub appearances (so its
alteration here lands as recognizable), fitting his established voice (gravelly,
commanding, a military mentor's cadence — see `voice-notes-draft.md`). Not
invented here since it's a real writing decision, not a structural one — flagged
for whoever drafts Max's actual dialogue lines.

## Confirmed (Orlando, 2026-09-16)

4. **Marcus keeps playing along** — he doesn't confront Max. He deliberately lets
   Max keep thinking the deception is working, specifically **to see how far Max
   will take it**. This gives Marcus a real information advantage over Max from
   this point forward (the player and Marcus both know something Max doesn't
   know they know), and sets up an eventual reckoning later rather than resolving
   it here. Writing guidance: every Max scene after Mission 3 needs to work on
   two levels — Max still believes his cover is intact, while Marcus is
   privately testing/watching him.

**Mission 3 is now fully drafted, all open questions resolved.**

## Beat shape (proposed, pending question 4 above)

1. **Setup** — Max sends Marcus after the decoy, at Max's workshop, using the
   speech-tell phrase during the hand-off — planted here so it can be echoed/
   recognized later. Continues the post-Mission-2 pattern of Max still acting the
   trusted mentor even though the player already knows better.
2. **The mission itself** — Marcus (and Cookie) pursue the decoy target, played
   straight as a real mission with real stakes. Per `story-bible.md`'s hindsight-
   clue rule, the player shouldn't be handed the reveal early — the decoy should
   feel legitimate in the moment.
3. **The discovery** — arriving at/completing the objective, something about it
   doesn't add up (consistent with a *planted* decoy, not a real target) —
   proposed as the moment Marcus mentally replays the hand-off and recognizes the
   phrase was said wrong. This is a sound-first realization, well-suited to an
   audio cue or a callback line rather than a visual one.
4. **Resolve hardens** — mirroring the opening scene's own "resolve hardens" beat
   (`01-opening-dream-and-waking.md`), Mission 3 ends on Marcus getting harder/
   more determined rather than breaking. Still open whether this resolve stays
   aimed purely at The Madame or now also includes reckoning with Max — tied to
   question 4 above.
