# Mission 2 (draft) — Finding the Resistance

Built from Orlando's answers (2026-09-16): Marcus has a lead out of Mission 1
pointing toward the resistance; Mission 2's shape is infiltrating them uninvited —
the resistance is secretive per `locations.md`, so Marcus has to find and break
into their hidden base before any conversation happens at all, rather than being
introduced or recruited. **Everything below the "Confirmed" line in each section is
Claude's proposed structure, not stated fact — correct freely**, same convention as
`02-mission-one-black-market.md`.

## Confirmed
- Marcus (with Cookie) follows the lead from Mission 1 toward the resistance.
- The resistance does not know he's coming and has not agreed to see him — this is
  an infiltration, not a meeting.
- Tonal shift from Mission 1: more stealth/investigation-led than the black
  market's search-and-confront structure.
- **The resistance's leader is Max Gomez** (confirmed 2026-09-16 — see the new
  MID-STORY REVEAL section in `max-gomez.md`). Marcus does not know this going in
  and is genuinely caught off guard when he finally confronts the leader and finds
  his own friend/mentor. This is Mission 2's actual climax and its central reveal.
- **This requires earlier content establishing Marcus and Max as friends first**
  (Orlando, 2026-09-16), in Max's known mentor/supplier persona, so the reveal
  lands as a shock rather than an introduction to a stranger. Where that content
  lives (folded into Mission 1, a bridging scene between missions, or its own
  content) is still open — see the question below.

## Sub-mission 1: Following the Signal

**Location:** New Carthage at night, a different stretch of the city than
Mission 1's route — proposed: somewhere less market/commerce-coded, more
industrial or residential-slum, matching the resistance's need to stay hidden.

**Beats:**
1. Marcus and Cookie work the lead from Mission 1 — proposed: this plays as
   tracking/investigation rather than a straight walk, e.g. following a signal,
   tailing someone connected to the resistance, or piecing together a location
   from what the Mission 1 contact gave up under pressure.
2. Cookie's guidance role (per `cookie.md`, still an unbuilt mechanic as of Mission
   1's draft) would get a second, more investigation-flavored use here if that
   mechanic exists by this point — proposed, not confirmed as a dependency.
3. Proposed complication: the resistance, being paranoid about infiltrators, may
   have their own watchers/lookouts on the approach — a stealth encounter that's
   about *avoiding detection* rather than fighting, tonally distinct from Mission
   1's corp-patrol encounters.

## Sub-mission 2: The Breach

**Location:** The resistance's hidden base — not yet given a physical description
beyond "tight-knit and secretive" in `locations.md`. Proposed: something
deliberately unglamorous and defensible — a converted industrial site, an
abandoned transit tunnel, a building that reads as nothing from outside. Open for
Orlando to specify or replace.

**Beats:**
1. Marcus and Cookie find a way in without an invitation — proposed as the
   mission's main stealth set-piece, testing the Acoustic Stealth Loop against
   defenders who are (unlike Aegis-Corvus patrols) not just faceless corp
   security, but people with their own cause.
2. Proposed tonal note: worth deciding whether resistance guards should feel like
   antagonists to fight through, or people Marcus is trying hard *not* to hurt,
   given they're likely future allies. This affects how combat here should feel
   compared to Mission 1's confrontation.

## Sub-mission 3: Discovered — and the Reveal

**Location:** Deeper inside the base, wherever the leader actually operates from.

**Confirmed beats:**
1. Marcus is found/confronted by resistance members before he can explain himself
   — proposed as a real fight or near-fight, using the same combat kit shown off
   in Mission 1 (cane, martial arts, Cookie attacking).
2. **The leader steps in — and it's Max.** This is the mission's actual climax.
   Marcus is genuinely blindsided; per Orlando, this only lands if the player has
   already spent real time with Max-as-friend earlier in the game (see the open
   structural question above).
3. **Mission ends on Max's reaction/explanation** — exact content still open (see
   question 3 above), but this is the hinge into Mission 3, replacing the more
   generic "resistance response" framing from the earlier draft.
