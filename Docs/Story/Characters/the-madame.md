# The Madame — Antagonist

Built from Orlando's story intake answers (2026-09-13). Voice notes still open — see
`Docs/Story/Dialogue/dialogue-intake-form.md`. Note: "The" is explicitly part of her
name/title, not an article in front of a title — always "The Madame," not "Madame X"
or similar.

## Role

Head of Aegis-Corvus Dynamics. Has ruled New Carthage with an iron fist for over 10
years, driven purely by profit, with no regard for ordinary people in the streets as
long as the money keeps flowing. The specific individual Marcus is hunting — his
revenge targets her personally, not the corporation as an abstraction.

## Physical description

Highly fashionable, dressed at the height of top fashion — always sharp, precise, and
put-together. Nothing about her appearance is ever out of place. (A useful contrast
point against New Carthage's dark, dingy, retro-future-modern interiors and Marcus's
own utilitarian tactical gear — she looks like she belongs to a different, cleaner
world than the one she's built.)

## The open question this file does NOT answer — do not resolve without Orlando

The story bible's ending involves **a familial revelation involving The Madame that
forces Marcus into a tough decision.** What that revelation actually is — how she's
connected to Marcus or to Desi, and why it complicates a straightforward revenge story
— is a real, structurally central creative decision that has not been made yet. This
file deliberately does not guess at it.

## Relationships

- **Marcus "Echo" Cross** — the target of his revenge; the exact nature of any deeper
  connection is the story's central unresolved question (see above).
- **Aegis-Corvus Dynamics** — founder and head.
