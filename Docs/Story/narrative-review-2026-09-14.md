## 2. Unresolved setups with no matching payoff

This is where the real findings are. Most dangling threads are dangling *by design* —
the ending and the middle missions simply aren't written yet — so those are listed
separately from the one that's actually a problem.

**Worth attention now:**

- **Marcus's augment-safety fear has no payoff anywhere.** This is the only setup
  planted in an actual *written scene* rather than in bible notes, which makes it the
  most exposed. Right now nothing in the story ever returns to it.
  **A possible payoff already exists in canon, and it's a good one:** the prophecy
  reveal says The Madame and G started believing because Marcus does things with his
  augments "nobody else could do." So what Marcus experiences as dangerous strain could
  actually *be* the bloodline manifesting through the cyberware — his body isn't
  failing, it's becoming something. That would convert a loose thread into a
  load-bearing one and tie the opening scene directly to the ending. **Offered as an
  option, not assumed** — it's exactly the kind of connective decision that should come
  from Orlando.
- **Desi's stolen research is in the premise but never revisited.** She's the stated
  motivation for everything, yet no written material does anything with the research
  itself. Combined with finding 3 below, there's a real risk she functions as a
  motivation object rather than a presence. Worth at least one mid-story beat that
  touches the research directly.

**Dangling by design (ending/middle unwritten), tracked not flagged:** Max's secret
(the father's assassination) needs a payoff scene; the twin reveal and vampire
bloodline need the ending; G needs at least one on-screen encounter given he's
established as a recurring-threat-caliber character; the resistance has a location and
a role but no scene and no named member; the bonus mission has no content.

## 3. Does the ending satisfy the resolution statement / has the story drifted?

**This is the most substantive finding.** The stated theme is *justice for Desi's death,
braided with the resistance exposing a decade of corporate exploitation*. The ending as
currently described — The Madame is Marcus's twin sister, the Cross bloodline descends
from vampires, a tough choice follows — is a payoff for a **family and identity** theme,
not obviously for the **justice/exploitation** theme the bible actually states.

That's not a flaw in the ending; the reveal is strong. It's a mismatch between the
stated theme and the stated ending, and there are three clean ways out:

1. **Restate the theme** to what the ending actually pays off (identity/blood vs. the
   family you lost — revenge curdling when the target turns out to be kin).
2. **Connect the corp plot to the bloodline** so both land at once — e.g. Aegis-Corvus's
   illegal bioweapons research (already canon as the reason Desi was killed and Marcus
   was blinded) was studying or weaponizing the bloodline's traits. This is already
   listed as an open question in the story bible and would make the justice theme and
   the supernatural reveal the *same* payoff rather than competing ones.
3. **Give the resistance a real stake in the climax** so the exploitation thread
   resolves on-screen rather than being background.

Option 2 looks strongest because it costs nothing structurally — the connective tissue
is already half-built and merely unstated.
