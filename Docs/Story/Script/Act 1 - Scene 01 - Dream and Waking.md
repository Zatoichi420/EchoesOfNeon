# Act 1 - Scene 01 - Dream and Waking

**Scene ID:** A1-S01 | **Act:** 1 | **Mission:** Prologue, launches Mission 1 | **Location:** the kitchen (dream), Desi's old lab (waking) | **Status:** CONFIRMED by Orlando 2026-10-08 (merged from Flanagan's draft plus notes from King, Lindelof, Martin, Smith, Bernardin). The three [ORLANDO DECIDES] items were all approved as written: the radio-tubes lead, the Inventory stated correctly now with the drift starting in Act 2, and the requisition cut with the date kept.
**Cast:** Marcus (Orlando; African-American, plain and dry) | Desi (separate actor; warm, quick; reverb in dreams only) | Cookie (separate actor; low, dry, close-mic, no reverb) | City PA (a measured corporate voice).
**Run time played:** about 8 minutes. Every stretch has a player action.

## SOUND: the dream
Kettle ticking as it cools, a toaster pops, a clock, one bird outside on the right. Pencil scratching, panned LEFT (Desi writes with her left hand). A cup set down, centre. [PLANT: dry vs reverb; every dream line from Desi carries long warm reverb, Marcus does not. Reverb is never used in present-day scenes.]
**MUSIC:** none. Only Desi's hum, unaccompanied.
**ACTION:** the player presses once to sit in the kitchen chair. The chair scrape is theirs, and the hard cut that follows is the result of their own action.

## LINES: the dream [SAFE]
DESI (reverb): You're doing that thing again. Thinking so loud I can hear it from here.
MARCUS (dry, Orlando): I'm listening to you eat toast.
[PERF] Close to the mic, easy pace, interrupt yourself once to chew.
DESI (reverb): Liar. *(her laugh, in two pieces: a small one, then the real one)*
MARCUS (dry): You've been up since four.
DESI (reverb): Since three. The numbers were being rude.
MARCUS (dry): That coffee's cold.
DESI (reverb): On purpose. Cold coffee is honest. Hot coffee is just anxious.
*(She starts to hum under her breath. On the sixth note the hum slips a half step flat, and she covers it with a cough. Marcus says nothing.)* [PLANT: the lying note, Desi's tune; the lock in Act 2 and the vault in Act 3.]
MARCUS (dry): You're humming.
DESI (reverb): I'm fine.
*(The chair is the player's. Desi's voice follows the scrape.)*
DESI (reverb): Sit. I'm about to tell you something, and I'd like you sitting down when you get annoyed.
DESI (reverb): It's not a disease, Marcus. It's not even
*(Hard cut. The reverb stops mid-word. The kettle's cooling tick keeps going for two seconds of dry silence, then does not continue in the waking room.)* [PLANT: the dream leaks one sound into the real room, wrong by a beat.]

## SOUND: waking
Rain on glass, RIGHT (the cold-blue window). A failing fan and a 60-cycle hum from the equipment rack, LEFT. A reel-to-reel motor idling and a slow tape hiss, AHEAD on the bench. A draught whistles under the door, BEHIND, with a loose-hinge tick. The bed spring creaks under him, so the way back is by ear. Low city drone behind the glass. Warm interior: a radiator tick, old wood. Through the window, far off: a ferry horn, two long and one short; a city PA ("Curfew lifts at the half hour. Aegis-Corvus thanks you for your patience.") repeated a beat later in Spanish and Tagalog, each ending on the same falling note; a bassline from a window below; a neighbor shouting down a stairwell, "Yalla, the tram!"
*(Cookie: tail thump, collar jingle, three paw taps on the floor.)*
COOKIE (low, dry, close): Marcus.
**ACTION:** the player may now move and use the sonar pulse (one button).

## LINES: waking [SAFE]
MARCUS (Orlando): Cookie. Hey, girl. What day is it? No. Don't tell me. Let me get there on my own.
[PERF] Rough with sleep, warm to the dog, a little proud on the last line. Pride is the grief.
*(He swings his legs off the bed and finds the nightstand: Desi's glasses, folded; a photograph with a thumb-worn corner. He does not pick them up.)*
MARCUS (Orlando): Morning. I think.

## ACTION: first pulse, the room by ear, and the strain
The player presses pulse. It returns in three bounces: the rack (left, a low metal hum, which drops a hair flat and recovers), the window (right, rain), the bench with the recorder (ahead, motor idle). Each pulse carries the first note of Desi's tune, very faint. [PLANT: the tune is a key; payoff Act 3.]
The player presses pulse a second time. This pulse stutters and cuts out halfway. A thin ringing fills his ears, his hand trembles, and Cookie presses against his leg. [The strain, felt, not only stated.]
MARCUS (Orlando): There it is again. Longer this time.
[PERF] Level, not frightened aloud. A craftsman checking a failing tool.
MARCUS (Orlando): Something in this rig's cooking. I feel it in my back teeth.
[PERF] He believes it. Do not make him suspicious of anything else.

## PUZZLE: Find the Bench
Listen for the motor idle ahead. Hint 1: Cookie's collar jingle moves toward the bench and waits. Hint 2: Marcus says "Reel's ahead, on the bench." Hint 3: the sonar pulse lights the bench return and Cookie leads him in. No fail state.
*(Cookie crosses the room but goes around one stretch of floor by the door, her pawsteps curving. A floorboard creaks where she avoids.)*
[PLANT: the stretch is where Desi fell; Cookie only reacts, never explains.]
MARCUS (Orlando): Long way round again, Cook.
[PERF] Routine. He has noticed every morning and has never asked.
*(She says nothing and keeps going.)*

## ACTION and CLUE: the chalkboard (three touch zones, any order)
His smart glasses read the board back to him in their own flat voice, then he repeats what matters. Dust is gritty under his fingertips. Three zones, one reading each:
- Zone 1, the diagrams. GLASSES (flat): Two diagrams. A. B, with a question mark. MARCUS: A note in the margin I can't make out.
- Zone 2, the sentence. GLASSES: "...it's not a disease. It's not even." Then an eraser smear. MARCUS: I read it every day. It never gets easier to not understand.
- Zone 3, the numbers. GLASSES: Numbers, circled twice. Twelve, four, seventy-eight. MARCUS: Doesn't mean anything to me. Never has.
[PERF] Slow, reading aloud like a prayer he doesn't believe in yet.
[PLANT: Subject A / B (?) and the unfinished sentence pay off at the reveal and the Act 3 playback; 12/04/78 is a code, the "doctor's door"; the torn Aegis-Corvus requisition (cut from this scene) is read later.] **[ORLANDO DECIDES: the requisition is cut from Scene 01 to avoid overload; Bernardin also suggested cutting the date, but the date is how the player learns the code. Keep both the date and the requisition cut?]**

## LINES: the Inventory [SAFE]
[CANON 2026-10-16: the solder line establishes, in Act 1 and in plain view, that Desi was Max's niece. Marcus has always known; the player must too (fair play).]
**ACTION:** triggered when the player touches the photograph on the nightstand.
MARCUS (Orlando): Desi. Five six. Five seven in the boots she hated. Glasses up in her hair, and she'd tear the house apart looking for them. Coffee cold, on purpose. A laugh in two pieces, the little one first, like she was checking the room was safe. Left-handed. Smeared everything she wrote. Her uncle taught her to solder. She was better at it than him. She snored. Small, offended engine. *(under his breath)* I'd never say that to her face.
[PERF] A catechism, quick and sure. The last line is private and tender. [PLANT: the facts here are correct; the drift begins in Act 2, per Lindelof.] **[ORLANDO DECIDES: Flanagan and Smith had Marcus correct himself here ("No. Her right.") which spends the drift in the first minute; Lindelof and I recommend stating it correctly now and starting the drift in Act 2.]**

## SOUND and LINES: the reel
**ACTION:** the player threads the recorder and presses record. He speaks to tomorrow's Marcus. The player presses stop.
*(He first threads an earlier tape. A tape hisses, then his own voice, old and tired.)*
MARCUS (recorded, Orlando): ...radio tubes. Ask who still rebuilds the ones that shouldn't work. I don't know why I wrote that down. I wrote it so you'd have it.
*(He stops it. A clean half second of silence. Then a glass tapped, three quick, one slow, before the click.)*
[PLANT: he taps in his sleep; he does not hear it; the Madame listens to these tapes; the proposed lead is a trade clue that points to Roz's stall without the password.] **[ORLANDO DECIDES: confirm the lead (the radio tubes) as the Mission 1 lead.]**
*(Cookie's collar stops. She goes still near the recorder.)*
*(He starts a new tape. Player presses record.)*
MARCUS (Orlando, into the recorder): Tomorrow's Marcus. Eat. Feed Cookie before you go.
MARCUS (Orlando, into the recorder): Take the cane, not the spare. Market first. Ask about the radio tubes.
MARCUS (Orlando, into the recorder): And say her name out loud so you remember what this is for. The Madame.
*(Player presses stop. The hiss ends half a second late, as if something finished listening. Then the click.)* [PLANT: the building listens.]
MARCUS (Orlando): Everybody blames the company. Fine. A company's a building. She's the one who signs.
[PERF] Quiet and flat. The fury is in the control. He says "she" as if it were a door he has decided to open.
*(Alternate line for Orlando: "Everybody says untouchable like it's weather. It's a woman. Women have addresses.")*
*(The player holds a button to hum. He hums Desi's tune alone. Cookie whines once on a single note. The player releases.)*
MARCUS (Orlando): Close enough, Cook.
**MUSIC:** a thin, held tone under the last line, then silence.

## ACTION: launch
**ACTION:** the player opens the door: three bolts, rain, the street. A faint off-pitch generator hum swells ahead and to the right, with frying hiss under it (the market). Far off, a billboard strikes three bright bell hits and waits. In the held gap Marcus's cane taps the doorframe three times and rests, unnoticed. The fourth note never comes. [PLANT: the crest's sonic logo with its withheld fourth tone.] Cookie's tail thumps once; her three paw taps cue the first step. **Mission 1 begins.**
