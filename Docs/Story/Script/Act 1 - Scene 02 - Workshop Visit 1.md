# Act 1 - Scene 02 - Workshop Visit 1

**Scene ID:** A1-S02 | **Act:** 1 | **Mission:** Prologue, launches Mission 1 | **Location:** Max's builder's workshop, the slums | **Status:** CONFIRMED by Orlando 2026-10-08 (Smith's draft; the bridge story and baker joke remain placeholder wording that Orlando may replace with a story of his own)
**Cast:** Marcus (Orlando) | Max (separate actor; Guatemalan, 60s, gravelly, a military mentor's cadence) | Cookie (separate actor; low, dry, close-mic, no reverb; no spoken lines here).

## SOUND: the workshop
Four landmarks, all by ear.
1. **The door, behind him:** three heavy bolts thrown one after another, then the hiss of a seal. After it closes the city drone is gone. (This is a dead-signal room; Marcus's sonar pulse returns clean, without the faint carrier hum of the Lantern Post scanners he hears everywhere else in the city.) MARCUS: Quiet in here. I forget how loud the city is. [PLANT: the scanner hum is the city's surveillance; the workshop is the one room it does not reach; the elder's eye reaches Max only outside it.]
2. **The soldering iron, RIGHT, at the bench:** a steady tick and a thin sizzle every few seconds.
3. **The parts bins, LEFT:** a loose rattle of screws and relays when Max reaches into them.
4. **The clock, AHEAD on the far wall:** it rings on the hour with uneven pitches. A low cluster of twelve soft strokes, a rest, four rising, a rest, then a pattern of seven high strokes and eight lower ones. [PLANT: 12 / 04 / 78 as a pitch pattern; Desi's "doctor's door" code; Max recognises it as his old unit's sign. The game never explains it here.]
Under it all: rain on a tin roof, overhead, steady.
**MUSIC:** none. Max hums nothing.

## ACTION: arrival
The player walks in from the door with Cookie leading to the bench by the soldering tick. Max's step is audible before he speaks: step, drag, step. His eyepatch servo whirs softly when he turns his head.

## LINES: arrival and the first fix [SAFE]
MAX: You eat today?
MARCUS: I had coffee.
MAX: That is not a meal. Eat. Then I show you the new emitter. In that order.
MARCUS: Your niece used to ask me that.
MAX: She learned it from me. Eat.
[CANON 2026-10-16: Desi was Max's sister's daughter. Max still never says her name; "your niece" is Marcus's word, not his.]
[PERF] Gruff, no warmth performed. The care is in the order of the sentences.
*(Cookie crosses and leans against Max's leg. He lets her. A hand scratches behind her ear.)*
MAX: She is thin. Did you feed her?
MARCUS: Before the tape.
MAX: Good. *(a grunt)* The knee is bad today. Rain. I should have been a baker.
[PERF] Dry, self-aimed, quick. Do not let him land a joke.
*(He sets down a thermos. Marcus hears him pour. A cup is pushed into his hand.)*
MAX: It is not good coffee. It is hot.

## ACTION: the upgrade bench
A spoken menu, three stations, navigated by direction. Each choice confirms by sound. Max speaks the options once, then waits.
**Station 1, the cane (on the bench, ahead):** "Flash. Blade. Emitter."
- Flash: a capacitor whines up, then a dry click.
- Blade: the cane's nested segments fold and lock with a ratchet and a snap into a short katana, then fold back.
- Emitter: a low hum rising into a clean tone.
**Station 2, the augments (RIGHT, on the rack):** "Gain. Range."
- Gain: Max says "Gain's capped. I turned it down. It was cooking you." *(a click)* [PLANT: first listen is concern; second listen is the jailer's hand. Do not name it.]
- Range: a long pulse that rolls away into the tin.
**Station 3, Cookie (LEFT, by the bins):** "Collar clasp. Emitter."
- Clasp: a brass snap.
- Emitter: a short high chirp, then Cookie's collar answers.
The player picks one upgrade at the bench and leaves with it.

## LINES: while he works [SAFE]
MAX: How do you sleep?
MARCUS: In pieces.
MAX: What do you remember?
MARCUS: You mean today?
MAX: I mean whatever you want to tell me.
[PLANT: Max asks about sleep and memory, carefully. He never asks about the night of the attack. He never asks why Marcus wants the Madame.]
*(A drawer on the far side of the bench rattles. Marcus's fingers brush the front of it. Max's palm lands flat on it with a soft slap.)*
MAX: Do not. That one sticks.
[PLANT: the locked drawer on a string. Payoff: the signet ring, later. Max wears the key on a string; Cookie goes still near the drawer.]
*(The key string clinks against Max's chest as he turns.)*
MARCUS: You ever lose a day, Max? Not a bad day. A day. I got a hole where Tuesday was.
[PERF] Quiet, curious, not frightened. A man mentioning a stain on a shirt.
*(A half beat of silence. The soldering iron ticks once. Max sets something down.)*
MAX: De... Hand me that driver. The long one.
[PERF] He starts her name on the first syllable and stops (one of two audible stops; the second is in Scene 07). Otherwise Max does not hesitate loudly. He just changes the subject, and he is a half beat late.
[PLANT: Max stops, then changes the subject. Payoff: he knows what Tuesday was.]
MARCUS: It's in your hand.
MAX: So it is.

## LINES: the bridge story [SAFE]
MARCUS: Tell me the bridge one.
MAX: You have heard it.
MARCUS: Tell it anyway.
MAX: *(a half laugh)* We cross a rope bridge in the rain. I am carrying a radio, you are carrying a dog we did not have yet. No. That is the wrong story.
*(He starts over, slow, in order.)*
MAX: We cross a rope bridge in the rain. My knee goes. You catch the strap with two fingers. You say, "Not today, Max." I say, "You do not get to decide that." And you say, "Watch me."
[PLANT: he tells it correctly and too carefully, like reading. Payoff: Mission 3, a rehearsed Max is recognisable.]
MARCUS: That was good.
MAX: It was a long time ago.
[PERF] The half laugh must sound real. The second telling must sound like reading.

## ACTION: eye at the threshold [AUDIO-ONLY]
Max crosses to the door, step, drag, step. Cookie goes still and turns her head toward the bolts. At the threshold a very faint hum begins, and his eyepatch servo ticks twice.
MAX: I check the rain.
*(Three seconds. The door seals again. Max returns.)*
[PLANT: the eye only works outside; the workshop itself is dead-signal. Do not name why. Max answers a half beat late once, right after he steps back in.]

## LINES: the slip and the goodbye
MARCUS: How long have I been coming here?
MAX: Long enough that you track mud on my floor. Mira, patojo, you take more than you tell me.
[PERF] Tired, tender, caught. This is the one Spanish slip of the scene. Do not repeat it.
*(Marcus tests the cane against the floor. Max throws the three bolts back.)*
MAX: Walk it in, walk it out, brother.
[PERF] The TRUE version: tired, ritual. A catch of breath after "in". A knuckle tap on the doorframe on "out". A weary "brother". Record it with the catch.
[PLANT: the Mission 3 tell is the rehearsed version, clean, a quarter beat too fast, no catch, no tap.]
MARCUS: Walk it out.

## ACTION: launch
Marcus leaves with the upgrade chosen. The rain outside is louder. Cookie leads. **Mission 1 begins.**
