# Act 2 - Scene 08 - Following the Signal

**Scene ID:** A2-S08 | **Act:** 2 | **Mission:** 2, sub-mission 1 (Following the Signal) | **Location:** the Foundry Belt waterfront, to the Cistern's gate | **Status:** CONFIRMED by Orlando 2026-10-08 (Martin's draft, no changes)
**Cast:** Marcus (Orlando) | Cookie (separate actor; low, dry, close-mic; one word, "Listen.") | Wick (Vietnamese, young lookout, fast and nervous; distant calls only) | Ferro (Brazilian, lookout on a crane, slow and heavy; distant calls only) | a welder (Tamil English, marker "da") | a canal boatwoman (Bengali English, marker "only") | Marcus's smart glasses (flat voice).
**Run time played:** about 8 minutes. Every stretch has a player action. No Aegis patrols and no barks in this scene; the danger is the lookouts.

## SOUND: the waterfront
Landmarks, all by ear.
1. **The canal, LEFT:** a slow lap against iron, a hollow knock where a moored hull shifts.
2. **A crane's chain, FAR RIGHT and high:** a long creak, a rest, a clink.
3. **A pump station's low drone, AHEAD:** one flat note, steady, felt in the chest. It grows as he nears.
4. **Wet iron underfoot:** plates ring, gratings rattle, planks hollow.
5. **The Lantern Post hum, overhead:** thick near the first posts, thinner as he walks, and at the fence it stops. [PLANT: a place the city's scanners do not reach.]
Far behind, a billboard strikes three bright bell hits and waits. They do not come again.
**MUSIC:** none.

## ACTION: leaving the market edge
Cookie leads. The player sweeps the cane in two points, left and right, and reads the ground by tap: a plate rings, a grating rattles, a puddle slaps, the canal edge gives a long drop with no echo. Marcus holds the bell pattern by saying it.
MARCUS (Orlando): Two long, one short. Two long... one...
*(He stops. The canal laps. Nothing comes.)*
MARCUS (Orlando): Two long, one short. There.
[PERF] Light, a man checking a pocket he knows is empty. No fear. [PLANT: the amnesia leak; the bell is the only thing he has to hold, so he says it aloud.]

WELDER: Mind the spark, da. I am only patching.

BOATWOMAN: It is a bad night to be walking this side. No boots. Even the company does not come here. They say the water bites.
[PERF] Marcus is courteous and quick. He files the line, "even the company does not come here."

## PUZZLE: The Lamp Bell
At a junction of three pipes the bell tolls inside the iron: two long, one short. Three branches carry it, LEFT, CENTRE and RIGHT.
- **Left branch:** the toll smears into a wash of noise, long and soft.
- **Centre branch:** the toll doubles on itself, a stutter, no clean rest.
- **Right branch:** the rhythm stays crisp, and a thin echo follows each toll by a steady lag, never blending.
**Solution:** the true branch is where the rhythm stays constant while the echoes lag behind it.
- **Hint 1:** Cookie tilts her head toward the pipes. COOKIE (low, dry, close): Listen.
- **Hint 2:** as the player turns toward the right pipe the bell rises a half step in pitch.
- **Hint 3:** Cookie leads to the junction and stops, turned back toward him.
**No fail state.** The wrong branch dead-ends at a grating and the bell goes dull; Cookie waits for him to return.
MARCUS (Orlando): That one holds its beat.
[PERF] Quiet, a craftsman hearing a good joint. He does not name what the bell is.

## SOUND: the pump station nears
The drone thickens. Cookie's pace slows; the collar jingle thins to a rustle. At the edge of the drone she stops dead, head up, still.
*(No growl. No whine. Her breath is the only thing that moves.)*
[PLANT: Cookie knows a hum; she never says so. Payoff: Desi's lab, the Cistern, Act 3.]
MARCUS (Orlando): Cook. You know this one?
*(She says nothing. After a long second the jingle returns, slow, and she leads on.)*
[PERF] Gentle, not asking twice.

## PUZZLE: The Lookout Count
Open walkway between two stacks of crates, twelve metres of exposed iron. High on a warehouse roof, left, WICK taps a pipe rail; on a crane cab, far right, FERRO answers. The taps travel along the pipes in cycles.
- **One tap:** a boat or a stray; ignore it.
- **Two taps:** someone is moving; hold.
- **Three taps:** clear; they have looked away.
Wick's calls are quick and thin; Ferro's are slow and heavy.
WICK (far, high, fast): Two! Somebody on the walk! *(pipe: tap, tap)*
FERRO (far, slow): Is a blind man. Hold. *(pipe: tap, tap)*
*(A rest. Rain. The crane chain clinks. Then three taps from the roof and three from the crane.)*
- **Hint 1:** Cookie's jingle drops to a rustle on a two and returns on a three.
- **Hint 2:** MARCUS (Orlando): "Two, somebody's looking. Three, they've looked away."
- **Hint 3:** Cookie crosses on the three and waits behind the second stack; the player follows her jingle.
[PERF] Hint 2 is a man talking himself through a count. Calm, low, one breath.
**No fail state, no timer.** If the player crosses on a two:
WICK (far, high, fast): That's him! The one from the market. The Echo!
FERRO (far, slow): No blood on the waterfront. Lose him. Do not hurt him.
*(Three short whistles. Posts shift on the roof and the crane. Cookie turns him back to the last crate stack, and the cycle begins again. Nothing is lost.)*
[PLANT: the lookouts are future allies; they avoid hurting him; they do not know why he has come; no one says who leads them.]

## CLUE: the fence and the sign
The Lantern Post hum thins, flickers, and stops at a rusted chain-link fence. The silence is a pressure on the ears. The canal laps. The pump drone is loud now, a body in the air.
*(Marcus's hand finds a gate: rust crumbles under his palm, a chain, a padlock hanging open.)*
GLASSES (flat): Sign. Danger. Toxic spill. Restricted area. Aegis-Corvus Environmental Compliance. Keep out.
MARCUS (Orlando): No smell.
[PERF] Flat, a man noting a missing sound.
MARCUS (Orlando): A spill that old rots the air. And nothing stinks.
*(He sniffs. Rust, wet iron, algae. Nothing sour.)*
MARCUS (Orlando): Somebody wrote that sign to keep people out.
[PLANT: the falsified spill record; do not state who wrote it.]
*(No boots anywhere. No flat voices. The only sounds are water, iron and the drone.)*
MARCUS (Orlando): Nobody patrols a poison. *(a beat)* Nobody patrols a lie.
[PERF] A sentence he did not plan. He does not repeat it.

## ACTION: the Cistern's door
Past the gate, down a short ramp. The drone becomes a wall of sound. The ground is wet concrete; the ramp ends at a steel door, sweating condensation, beaded rivets under his fingers.
*(Cookie stops and sits. The collar settles. She is turned toward the door, and her head is lowered.)*
MARCUS (Orlando): Here.
*(He flattens a hand against the steel. Inside, far below, water moves in a huge empty room.)*
MARCUS (Orlando): Is the ferry running?
*(Nothing. The drone. Rain. He raises his knuckles.)*
MARCUS (Orlando): Two.
**The knock puzzle begins.** (Scene 09 follows.)
