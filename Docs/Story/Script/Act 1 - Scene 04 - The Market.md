# Act 1 - Scene 04 - The Market

**Scene ID:** A1-S04 | **Act:** 1 | **Mission:** 1, sub-mission 2 (Finding the Contact) | **Location:** the black market, under the Foundry Belt overpass | **Status:** CONFIRMED by Orlando 2026-10-08 (King's draft with three continuity fixes)
**Cast:** Marcus (Orlando) | Cookie (separate actor; one short line only) | Pike (Cape Town English, dry, slow) | Mama Ines (Guatemalan; K'iche' and Spanish, warm gravel) | Kit (Filipino, 12, bright, bored) | Dace (Jamaican, smooth, fast) | Dobbs (Trinidadian, polite, flat) | Wren (Samoan, New Zealand English, hoarse) | Kessel (Afrikaans-English, loud, sweet) | Vance (Haitian, thin, too polite) | Roz Adeyemi (Lagos English, 57, brisk; the last beat only) | the elder (radio; measured, aged).
**Run time played:** about 8 minutes. Every stretch has a player action.

## SOUND: the market by ear
Layers, bottom to top. An off-pitch diesel generator, LEFT and low (louder toward the entrance, lower deeper in). Rain on corrugated awnings and drips into pots. A warped-jazz radio loop. A frying hiss and the chatter of the FRYING STALL, CENTRE. A cough. A child laughing far off. A wind chime of loose screws, FAR RIGHT, marking the back alley. [Three landmarks: generator, frying stall, chime.]
A brass bell rings twice, a pause, a third ring, whenever someone passes the back alley. [PLANT: the contact's bell; the puzzle below.]
**MUSIC:** none. The jazz loop is in the world.
**ACTION:** Cookie sits at the entrance and the collar settles. The player may walk any stall in any order. The lead: Marcus asks each stall the same thing.
MARCUS (Orlando): Radio tubes. Who still rebuilds the ones that shouldn't work?
[PERF] Flat and patient, the same sentence every time. He is not asking twice; he is asking each stall.

## LINES: the stalls [SAFE]
**Kessel (frying stall).** Marcus asks.
KESSEL: Hot bao, hot bao, nobody leaves hungry! Eat, then ask. Lekker, it is. People talk better fed.
[PERF] Loud, sweet, a man who loves his work.

**Dace (augment dealer).**
DACE: Tubes? Nobody's asked since the flood. *(a sales laugh)* Good glass, honest wire. Not Aegis quality, I'll say it first. You want Aegis quality, go work for Aegis. This yaad is for people who can't.
MARCUS: I'll keep it in mind.
[PERF] Fast, smooth, a closer's rhythm. He is honest in the way that sells.

**Pike (rumor vendor).** A coin clinks into a tin.
PIKE: Now-now, that costs you. Rumors are two coins. You want the other kind, the useful kind, that is three.
MARCUS: The first kind.
PIKE: Birds went quiet on Delancey Row. Not flew off. Went quiet. Every bird, same minute.
PIKE: Dogs won't cross the Marlow courtyard. Not for meat. Not for money.
PIKE: And there's a man nobody has heard breathe. Not at the stall, not at the tram. They say he walks on the quiet side of a room.
[PLANT: three of G's uncanny tells as city gossip; never named. Payoff: the awning scene and the Tower.]
*(Cookie's collar stops for a beat, then resumes.)*
MARCUS: Who rebuilds them?
PIKE: Ah. That one is three coins. *(a pause)* I'll say it for free if you ask me twice.
[PERF] Dry, slow, a man selling secrets by the ounce.

**Wren (chemist).**
WREN: Don't touch the jars. Don't breathe near the jars. Somebody was asking about a blind man. Not me. I didn't say.
[PERF] Hoarse, anxious, a cough on "jars".

**Vance (patrol informant).**
VANCE: I didn't see anything. I'm very good at not seeing. You have until the sweep.
*(A pair of boots, far off, steady.)*
COOKIE (low, dry, close): Boots. Two. Wait.
*(No timer runs. The boots pass at the edge of the market and fade; the jazz loop swells back in. [PLANT: the patrol is pressure, not a fail state.])*

## LINES: the old woman [SAFE]
A hand finds Marcus's sleeve, light and sure. A rustle of wool, a smell of masa.
INES: I know your walk. The Doctor's husband.
*(Marcus stops.)*
MARCUS: You knew her.
INES: She mended my son's hand when I could not pay. *(a K'iche' word under her breath)* She hummed while she worked. Never the same tune twice. Except one.
[PLANT: Desi's one fixed tune; the hum he does not remember. Payoff: the counter-key.]
MARCUS: Which one?
INES: Ay. If I could hum it, I would not be old.
[PERF] Warm, with a smile at the end. She is not sad; she is glad to have been asked.
INES: You want the man who keeps a bell, not a name. Listen to the alley. Twice, then a pause, then once.
[Hint tier 1.]

## LINES: the child [SAFE]
A bright voice counts out loud as she walks, a clap on every fourth step.
KIT: One, two, three, four. *(clap)* You lost?
MARCUS: Looking for someone.
KIT: Everyone's looking for someone. It's a market, po. Two coins and I'll walk you.
*(She claps a rhythm, small and quick.)*
KIT: One for the door, two for the stair, three for the closet, and four... *(slow)* ...is... there.
*(Marcus stops. The market goes on, and his breath stops with it.)*
*(Cookie presses against his leg. A nudge, then another.)*
MARCUS: That rhyme.
*(A long second. Water drips into a pot.)*
MARCUS: No. Nothing. Move.
[PERF] The first two words are real. The rest is Marcus reversing course. Do not explain the pause. [PLANT: the closet rhyme as a childhood anchor; Cass's counting memory pays off at the Tower.]
KIT: You okay? You're making a face.
MARCUS: I'm fine.

*(She leads him through the stalls by a short gap, skipping two vendors. Her counting is a tag, not a guide, so the player can keep moving by her steps.)*

## LINES and ACTION: Dobbs's radio [SAFE]
A stall with a radio, volume low. The jazz loop ends and a measured voice begins, aged, slow, with a faint wet click at the end of a long breath.
ELDER (radio): Good evening. The city is a patient. Patients do not choose. Rest now.
*(A glass is tapped, three quick, one slow. A chorus of staff voices, flat, falling.)*
STAFF (radio): Yes, sir.
*(Marcus's cane taps the stone, three quick, one slow, in answer. He does not notice. His pulse steadies. For half a second the tension bed of the scene falls quiet and his guard drops; he is almost sleepy.)*
*(Cookie's collar stops. A low growl, then nothing.)*
DOBBS: Evening hour, friend. We keep it low. Yes, sir, yes, sir. Ent?
MARCUS: Sure.
[PERF] Marcus is half a beat slow and easy, like a man hearing a lullaby he thinks is his own.
[PLANT: the tap as lock; the elder's evening hour; payoff at the Tower, when the cane answers before he decides.]

## PUZZLE: the Bell
The bell rings twice, a pause, a third ring each time someone passes the back alley. It is far, behind the wind chime.
- **Hint 1:** Ines says the contact "keeps a bell, not a name."
- **Hint 2:** Cookie gives a soft double-chirp when Marcus faces the right way.
- **Hint 3:** Pike, for a coin: "Follow the bell with the broken ring. Right, past the wind chime."
**No fail state.** The bell never stops; a wrong turn only costs a few steps.

## ACTION: arrival at Roz's stall
The wind chime passes overhead and fades. The bell is close now: brass, with a flat third ring. A stall of cables, relay boxes, a smell of solder and warm dust. A voice, Lagos English, brisk.
ROZ: You have been two stalls early since the gate. Sit.
*(A stool scrapes. Cookie is called with a click of the tongue. Marcus feels the dog lean into a hand.)*
ROZ: This clasp is hanging by one thread, you know. A dog like this, with a clasp like this, will be lost on a bad night.
*(A small tool, a snap, a click. The collar settles.)*
ROZ: There. Now ask me.
MARCUS: Radio tubes. Who still rebuilds the ones that shouldn't work?
*(She doesn't answer at once. The solder iron ticks.)*
[PERF] Roz is friendly and watchful. She is already deciding. This hands off to Scene 05.
