# Act 1 - Scene 03 - Night Streets

**Scene ID:** A1-S03 | **Act:** 1 | **Mission:** 1, sub-mission 1 (Night Streets, the movement and Cookie tutorial) | **Location:** the Understory, then the Foundry Belt edge, to the market | **Status:** CONFIRMED by Orlando 2026-10-08 (Bernardin's draft; the street voices are placeholders that may take more heritages)
**Cast:** Marcus (Orlando) | Cookie (separate actor; low, dry, close-mic, no reverb) | Aegis-Corvus patrol guards (flat, conditioned) | City PA | a street sweeper (Lagos English idiom).
**Run time played:** about 7 minutes. Every stretch has a player action. Written for the Emitter upgrade; variants are noted where the choice from Scene 02 changes a sound.

## SOUND: outside the workshop
Four landmarks, all by ear.
1. **A broken water pipe, LEFT:** a steady two-beat drip into a puddle.
2. **The Lantern Post scanner hum, overhead:** a thin carrier tone that thickens under each post and thins between. [PLANT: the city's sensor net; the workshop is the one room it does not reach.]
3. **Rain on wet cobble underfoot,** with a metal grate every few metres.
4. **Far ahead and right:** an off-pitch generator, faint. (The market.)
The city PA, in English and then Arabic, each ending on the same falling note: "Curfew lifts at the half hour. Aegis-Corvus thanks you for your patience." A billboard strikes three bright bell hits and waits. [PLANT: the fourth tone withheld.]

## ACTION: cane, pulse and Cookie (no spoken tutorial)
The player sweeps the cane left and right. Each surface has its own tap: wet stone rings, a grate rattles, a puddle slaps. The pulse button returns walls as tone, and each pulse carries the first note of Desi's tune. Cookie leads automatically, a few metres ahead, in 3D: pawsteps and a collar jingle. If the player falls behind she stops and waits turned back, and the jingle drops to a quiet rustle. If the player wanders she comes back, the jingle closing in, until a nose touches a hand. When they arrive she stops.

## LINES: the walk [SAFE]
*(At the pipe, Cookie's steps curve left.)*
MARCUS (Orlando): Left at the water pipe. I've walked this a hundred times.
*(He follows. Her steps lead on.)*
MARCUS (Orlando): Good girl. Thank you.
[PERF] The "thank you" is the real line. Say it as to a colleague, not a pet. She does not answer.

*(If the player lags, her steps stop and the jingle becomes a rustle.)*
MARCUS (Orlando): Yeah. I hear you. Coming.
*(If the player wanders, she comes back and her nose finds his hand. He lets her turn him.)*
*(Streets of many voices: a noodle cart calls, "Two minutes, bowl's hot," in a Cantonese lilt; a tram announcer says "Foundry Belt, next"; two kids argue in Tamil and English over a radio. Nobody looks up when the cane passes. They have learned not to.)*

## ACTION and PLANT: counting doors
Five doors on the right before the corner, each with its own sound: hollow wood, steel shutter, glass, hollow wood, a tin awning.
MARCUS (Orlando): Count them to the corner. Wood. Shutter. Glass. Wood... *(a tram passes)* Was that four? Or five.
*(Cookie stops. The jingle drops to a rustle. She waits, turned back.)*
MARCUS (Orlando): Where was I.
[PERF] Light, a man dropping a coin. No fear, no explanation. [PLANT: the amnesia leak; the player can re-count by cane in any order.]
*(He starts again. Her paws tap twice at the corner.)*
A SWEEPER (Lagos English): Eh, this rain is wahala, oga. Mind the grate.
MARCUS (Orlando): Thank you.
[PLANT: the city creole, one word in this scene.]

## PUZZLE: Walk on the Cup
At the Foundry Belt edge Cookie stops short.
COOKIE (low, dry, close): Three ahead. Two walking, one still. Wait.
**ACTION:** the player holds position and listens. The two walkers' steps alternate. The still guard taps a tin cup every eight seconds, even and exact.
GUARD (flat, falling note): Sector four, quiet. Settled. *(a cup tap)*
- **Hint 1:** Cookie's rustle stops when both walkers face away, and the jingle moves on.
- **Hint 2:** MARCUS: "Cup. He taps it between rounds. Count it."
- **Hint 3:** Cookie crosses on the tap and waits behind the crates; the player follows her jingle.
**No fail state.** A wrong step wakes the still guard.
GUARD (flat): Contact! East side!
The walkers run east. Cookie leads west, around the block, and the player rejoins the hiding point. Nothing is lost.
[PLANT: conditioned patrols end on the same falling note, the cup tap is exact, and a one-second freeze falls at any chime.]

## ACTION: the first refusal
Past the post, one walker peels away.
GUARD (flat): Heard that. Checking the alley.
The player waits at the corner, listening for his boot.
**ACTION:** the player aims the cane by his steps and fires the Emitter. A low hum rises into a clean tone. The guard drops.
*(Variant, Flash: a capacitor whine, a dry click, and he stumbles blind. Variant, Blade: the blade stays seated; Marcus uses the haft.)*
EARPIECE (a dispatcher on the fallen guard's radio, flat, falling note): Down he goes. Settled.
*(The dispatcher hears the fall over his open mic and reports it in the same calm. Nobody comes. [PLANT: the conditioned say "settled" even about their own people.])*
*(Cookie steps forward. Her growl is low and ready. Lethal up close.)*
MARCUS (Orlando): No.
*(He kneels, longer than he needs to. His fingers find the pulse at the throat, then he rolls the man onto his side and tucks a sleeve under his head. He takes the earpiece and the radio battery. The tin cup rolls; his foot stops it.)*
MARCUS (Orlando): You'll wake with a headache and no story. That's the best I've got.
[PERF] Unhurried, a craftsman closing a job. Dignity here is patience. [PLANT: the first of two refusals; the third is the unfinished tap.]

## SOUND: arrival
Cookie stops and sits. The collar settles. Ahead: the off-pitch generator (left), a frying hiss (centre), and a wind chime far right. Far off, a vendor's radio plays a measured voice too faint to parse, then a single glass tap. [PLANT: the elder's evening hour; heard only as sound here.]
MARCUS (Orlando): Here. *(a beat)* Good girl.
**Mission 1, sub-mission 2 begins.**
