# Act 1 - Scene 05 - Roz

**Scene ID:** A1-S05 | **Act:** 1 | **Mission:** 1, sub-mission 3 (The Confrontation) | **Location:** the back room behind Roz's stall, the market | **Status:** CONFIRMED by Orlando 2026-10-08 (Smith's draft; the cane becomes a short katana, per Orlando)
**Cast:** Marcus (Orlando) | Cookie (separate actor; one short line only) | Roz Adeyemi (Lagos English, 57, gravelly alto; one marker, "ah ah", used once) | Themba (Zulu English, deep, slow, courteous menace; creole word "eish") | Selvam (Tamil English, quick, light; marker "no?").
**Run time played:** about 8 minutes. Every stretch has a player action. Written for the Emitter upgrade; variants noted.

## SOUND: the back room
Landmarks, all by ear.
1. **A soldering iron, RIGHT, ticking** (carried from her stall).
2. **A shelf of radio tubes, LEFT:** glass touching glass, a faint chime when anyone moves.
3. **A tin of coins on a steel desk, AHEAD:** she taps coins against it as she counts.
4. **A beaded curtain, behind:** the stall's noise drops to a mutter once it swings shut.
Rain on a tin roof, low. A ceiling fan with a dry tick.
**MUSIC:** none.

## ACTION: the back room
Roz leads. The curtain hisses behind. A match scrapes, burns, shakes out. [PLANT: she quit smoking and still reaches.]
ROZ: In here, love. Walls are thicker.
*(Cookie crosses and sits near the steel desk. Her collar settles.)*
MARCUS (Orlando): Radio tubes. Who still rebuilds the ones that shouldn't work?
ROZ: *(counting coins, tapping)* Four, five, six. Nobody asks that and wants a short answer.
MARCUS (Orlando): I don't need it short.
[PERF] Level, close to the mic, no edge yet.
*(Two sets of boots enter, one from the right, one behind. The curtain shifts.)*
THEMBA: Mama says you take too long, brother. Eish, we have a rule here.
SELVAM: We only want to talk, no? Easy, easy.
ROZ: Boys. Not yet.
*(She says it without hurry. They do not stop.)*

## ACTION: the fight
**The player holds still and listens. Stepping, scrape, a bell of glass on the left. Two enemies, two sides.**
1. **Themba, RIGHT.** His step is heavy, even. The player aims the Emitter by his stride and fires: a low hum rising into a clean tone, and his boots stagger. *(Variant, Flash: a capacitor whine and a dry click; he swears in Zulu.)*
2. **Selvam, BEHIND.** A quick scuff, a blade drawn. Cookie, low and close:
COOKIE (low, dry, close): Left. Close.
3. **The cane becomes the blade.** Selvam's knife clears its sheath. Marcus thumbs the collar and the cane collapses on itself: a ratchet of nested segments folding down, a snap, a lock. It is now a short katana, straight and light, with a low singing note when it moves. One stroke rings Selvam's knife out of his hand; the return stroke stops a hair from his throat with a whistle and then nothing. He steps inside and throws him over his hip, a plain hip throw, and a sack of weight hits the floor. [PERF for the sound design: three strokes in under two seconds, each decisive. Deadly efficiency; he could end it and does not.]
4. Themba shakes it off and charges. The player parries with the short katana, a ring of steel on steel, cuts the cord of his belt and the ties of his sleeve in one pass, then drives the pommel into his ribs. He folds.
5. Selvam rolls up. Cookie closes in, a rush of paws and a snarl that fills the room. Her jaws take his sleeve, her weight pins him.
MARCUS (Orlando): Hold.
*(She stops, still on him, breathing hard. The growl drops to a rumble.)*
[PERF] Quiet. The command is a statement, not a shout. It is the first thing he has told her to do.
*(Selvam lies very still. Themba, on his knees, spits.)*
THEMBA: *(wheezing)* That dog is not a dog.
MARCUS (Orlando): She's a good listener. Both of you are going to be one now.
**No fail state.** If the player mistimes, a glancing hit rings his ear and the sequence plays through with the same result.
[PLANT: Cookie lethal up close but obedient; the first command Marcus gives her. Nobody dies.]
*(He nods at the door. Themba drags Selvam out. The curtain falls shut.)*
MARCUS (Orlando): Cookie. Let go.
*(She releases. The collar settles. The short katana folds back into the cane with a ratchet and a snap.)*

## LINES: the bargain [SAFE]
*(Roz is back at the desk. A coin taps, then another. The steel rings.)*
ROZ: I paid those two to keep out the Aegis, not the likes of you.
MARCUS (Orlando): They kept me out for nine seconds.
ROZ: Ah ah. You are rude and you are good. I will pay you not to be either.
MARCUS (Orlando): I'm not here for money.
ROZ: Everyone is here for money. That is what a market is, love.
*(A coin drops. She counts again from the start.)*
ROZ: I can tell you who sells good wire. I can tell you where the sweep turns. Not this.
MARCUS (Orlando): This.
ROZ: If I say it, they will know it was me.
MARCUS (Orlando): Who will?
ROZ: Anyone listening. Everyone listens.
MARCUS (Orlando): Then say it quietly.
*(Silence. Rain. The coins stop.)*
ROZ: Outside, the posts hear everything. In here only the walls hear. But walls tell.
MARCUS (Orlando): Roz.
*(Metal sings: he lays the short katana flat on the steel desk, edge toward her coin tin, the tip an inch from her coins. The blade rings once against the steel.)*
MARCUS (Orlando): Say it once. I lose things lately, so I'll make this one stick.
[PERF] Not a threat. A man asking for a loan he intends to repay. Do not raise the voice.
*(Roz breathes. The match scrapes again, burns, shakes out.)*
ROZ: You are the third man this year to ask me that so quietly. The other two are dead.

## PUZZLE: the signal
ROZ: Ferry. You ask, "Is the ferry running?" They answer, "Not since the flood." That is the words.
MARCUS (Orlando): And?
ROZ: And a knock on steel. Two. *(rest)* Three. Do not rush the rest.
*(She knocks it on the steel desk: two knocks, a long beat, three.)*
- **Hint 1:** Roz knocks it once, slowly.
- **Hint 2:** Cookie lifts a paw and taps the floor twice, then three times, waiting between.
- **Hint 3:** Marcus: "Two. Wait. Three. Wait longer than you think."
The player taps it back on the desk. A good rest earns a soft click from the desk. A rushed rest gets the same knock played back a half tone warmer, then a retry. **No fail state.** [PLANT: the knock is tested later at the Cistern door; the length of the rest is the test.]
MARCUS (Orlando): Again.
*(She does it again. He taps it back, right.)*
MARCUS (Orlando): I'll put it on a tape tonight.
ROZ: You are losing it, love. I can hear it.
MARCUS (Orlando): Where does it come from?
ROZ: From the ones who keep the lamps lit. That is all I know, and it is more than I should say.
[PLANT: the Lamplighters. Max is never named or implied.]
