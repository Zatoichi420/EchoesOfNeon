# Act 2 - Scene 07 - Workshop Visit 2 and the Dead Channel

**Scene ID:** A2-S07 | **Act:** 2 | **Mission:** Between Mission 1 and Mission 2 | **Location:** Max's workshop (Part A), then Desi's old lab (Part B) | **Status:** CONFIRMED by Orlando 2026-10-08 (Flanagan's draft with one line fixed)
**Cast:** Marcus (Orlando) | Max (separate actor; Guatemalan, 60s, gravelly, a military mentor's cadence; at most one Spanish slip: "vos") | Cookie (separate actor; low, dry, close-mic; one word at most) | Desi (separate actor; a present-day recording, dry, no reverb).
**Run time played:** about 10 minutes. Every stretch has a player action.

## SOUND: the workshop (same four landmarks as Scene 02)
1. **The door, behind:** three bolts, a seal hiss; then the city drone is gone and the sonar returns clean.
2. **The soldering iron, RIGHT:** a steady tick and a thin sizzle every few seconds.
3. **The parts bins, LEFT:** a loose rattle of screws and relays.
4. **The clock, AHEAD:** it strikes once, uneven, and goes on ticking.
Rain on tin, overhead. A land line telephone on the bench, a dry bell inside it.
**MUSIC:** none.

## LINES: arrival and the news [SAFE]
MAX: You are late. You eat?
MARCUS (Orlando): Not yet. Max, I have the signal for the lead.
[PERF] Level, close. He is carrying a dead woman and keeping it small.
MAX: Who gave it to you?
MARCUS (Orlando): Roz Adeyemi. Radio tubes. She's dead, Max.
*(A steel driver drops and rings across the concrete. Cookie lifts her head. The soldering iron ticks once, twice.)*
[PLANT: the first listen is grief, plain; the second listen is a man who already knew.]
MAX: *(very quiet)* She was a good cable woman.
[PERF] Do not break. The voice goes lower, not higher. It is a eulogy said to a bench.
MAX: Who gave you the signal word?
[PERF] A half second too fast, as if he was waiting to ask it.
MARCUS (Orlando): I told you. Roz.
MAX: Yes. Yes, you said.
[PLANT: Max is checking that Marcus followed the plant and no one else spoke to him.]
MARCUS (Orlando): They all stopped, Max. Six o'clock. Every patrol, for a second. Like someone said a word.
MAX: Shift change. Eat. *(a grunt)* Sit, vos. You are swaying.
[PERF] This is the scene's one Spanish slip, tired and tender. Do not repeat it.
*(A stool scrapes. A thermos is poured. A cup is put in Marcus's hand.)*

## ACTION: the retune
Max works at the bench, left, then right. A capacitor whines up and settles. The Emitter's tone rises, then drops into a damper, wetter timbre, with a slow tail that rolls away.
MAX: I retune the emitters for the rain. Water changes how they carry. Wet stone, wet metal.
MARCUS (Orlando): Since when does the rain matter?
MAX: Since it is always raining. Hold this.
[PLANT: the retune is for the Cistern's tunnels and water; do not state it.]
*(The land line rings, a dry bell. Max lifts it. The iron ticks.)*
MAX: *(into the phone, low)* Yes. Mm. Not now. *(pause)* Supplier.
[PERF] He says "supplier" the way a man says "weather". The tone is warm, an instruction, not a transaction.
*(He hangs up gently. The cradle clicks.)*
[PLANT: the tone never matches the word; this is a Lamplighter on the line.]

## ACTION: the reel repair
Marcus puts the portable recorder on the bench. Max turns it over: screws, a pinch roller swapped with a soft click, a head wiped with an alcohol swab (the smell cuts the air).
MAX: The head was dirty, and the roller slips. A tape that stutters is the machine, not you.
MARCUS (Orlando): I know what it was.
[PERF] Quiet. He is not saying it was the machine.
*(The recorder motor spins up, smooth, a clean hiss.)*
MAX: Keep talking to it. Somebody ought to hear you.
[PERF] A plea. Do not sell it as wisdom. The pause before "hear you" is a held breath.
[PLANT: the workshop is the one place the elder cannot hear; the line is Max's confession in miniature.]

## LINES: the hole in the day [SAFE]
MAX: If you want to put the Tuesday on the bench, I listen. No tools.
MARCUS (Orlando): Not today.
MAX: Not today is a good answer.
*(He sets down the swab. A long beat. The iron ticks.)*
[PLANT: Max never says Desi's name, never asks about the night, never says he leads anything.]

## ACTION: the door
Max throws the three bolts back.
MAX: Walk it in, walk it out, brother.
[PERF] The TRUE version: a catch of breath after "in", a knuckle tap on the doorframe on "out", a weary "brother". Record it with the catch.
MARCUS (Orlando): Walk it out.
*(At the threshold a very faint carrier hum starts, and the eyepatch servo ticks twice. Max answers a half beat late.)*
MAX: Mind the step.
[AUDIO-ONLY] [PLANT: the eye only works outside; the half-beat lag is the elder's relay.]
**Cut to the lab.**

## SOUND: the lab
Rain on glass, RIGHT. The rack's hum, LEFT. The reel's idle, AHEAD on the bench. On the shelf BEHIND him, the lab radio hisses on a dead band, the same flat hiss it has had since Scene 01. Cookie's collar jingles near it. She whines, low, once.

## PUZZLE: the Dead Channel
Cookie stops by the radio, turns her head to it, and whines. The player can approach, then hold a button to hum Desi's tune near the radio.
- **First hum (the tune sung straight):** the radio gives back the first three notes, very quietly, then the static grows warmer. It does not get louder.
- **Correct input:** the tune with the sixth note a half step flat, the way Desi hummed it when she said she was fine.
- **Hint 1:** Cookie whines toward the radio, and her whine slips flat on the sixth beat.
- **Hint 2:** Marcus, quietly: "She always hummed to it. Even when she was fine."
- **Hint 3:** the radio plays the first six notes back, the sixth flat.
No buzzers. No timers. No fail state; wrong hums only warm the static.
[PLANT: the flat note is the lying note; it is the lock for the Annex vault in Mission 3.]
*(When the sixth note lands flat, the static drops away like a hand lifting. A relay clicks twice.)*

## SOUND and LINES: Desi's recording [SAFE]
A close, dry voice, tired and honest. Less sweet than the dream. Present-day, no reverb.
DESI (recorded): Marcus, if this is playing, it means I ran out of time to say it kindly.
[PERF] Dry, close, a woman recording at midnight with a cold cup in her hand. Do not perform grief.
DESI (recorded): Right. This radio. I wired it to the old relay so I could talk to you when I wasn't home. You said it was a toy. It's a toy that works.
DESI (recorded): Cookie took her first stairs today. I may have cried. Don't tell Marcus.
*(A small laugh. A breath.)*
DESI (recorded): Which is stupid, because you'll hear this. Hello, you.
DESI (recorded): The lab smells like solder and wet dog. I've stopped hearing it. Tell me you notice.
DESI (recorded): I keep humming that tune so I don't have to say the other thing out loud. You've noticed. You always notice.
DESI (recorded): Eat something. A real meal. Not coffee. I'll know.
DESI (recorded): I've put something where only someone who loved me could find it. Don't go looking angry. Go looking the way I'd go looking for you.
*(Under her voice, faint and steady, a bell pattern begins: two long, one short.)*
[PLANT: the Lamp Bell, Mission 2's first sound clue.]
DESI (recorded): The part I keep not saying, Marcus, is
*(The recording cuts mid-word. A single clean click.)*
[PERF] The cut must feel like tape ending, not a design choice.
*(The static does not return. In its place, the bell pattern, alone and clear, repeats twice, then fades.)*
MARCUS (Orlando): *(quietly)* Finish it.
*(Nothing. The rack hums. Rain on glass.)*
MARCUS (Orlando): Finish it, love.
[PERF] Not a plea. A request to a person who is not there. Let the silence after it hold.
COOKIE (low, dry, close): Marcus.
*(Her collar settles against his leg.)*

## ACTION: the reel and the lead
The player presses record on the mended recorder.
MARCUS (Orlando, into the recorder): Tomorrow's Marcus. She left something on the radio. It stopped in the middle. There was a bell under it, two long, one short. Roz said the ones who keep the lamps lit. Find the lamps.
*(The player presses stop. The hiss ends half a second late. Then the click.)*
[PLANT: the Madame listens.]
**Mission 2 begins** with the player following the Lamp Bell.
