# Act 2 - Scene 13 - The Decoy and the Tell

**Scene ID:** A2-S13 | **Act:** 2 | **Mission:** 3, sub-mission 1 (The Lie Unravels) | **Location:** Max's workshop, then the alley outside | **Status:** DRAFT (Lindelof seat; awaiting Orlando)
**Cast:** Marcus (Orlando) | Max (separate actor; Guatemalan, 60s, gravelly, a military mentor's cadence; at most one Spanish slip: "vos"; one city creole word: "chamba") | Cookie (separate actor; low, dry, close-mic; no spoken lines here).
**Run time played:** about 9 minutes. Every stretch has a player action. Nobody is hurt.
**Two-level rule from here on:** every Max line is a friend's line and a liar's line. Write and record it so it works as both.

## SOUND: the workshop (the same four landmarks)
1. **The door, behind:** three heavy bolts, then the hiss of the seal. The city drone and the Lantern Post scanner hum drop away; the sonar returns clean.
2. **The soldering iron, RIGHT:** a steady tick and a thin sizzle.
3. **The parts bins, LEFT:** a loose rattle of screws and relays.
4. **The clock, AHEAD on the far wall:** it ticks, uneven.
Rain on tin, overhead. Under everything, close to the player's own body, a faint motor whir and a soft tape hiss from inside Marcus's coat: the recorder, already running. (It is the only sound in the room that is his.)
**MUSIC:** none. The limp is the score: step, drag, step.

## LINES: arrival [SAFE]
*(The bolts. The seal. Max's step, drag, step. The servo whirs as he turns his head.)*
MAX: You came.
MARCUS (Orlando): You said tomorrow.
MAX: I did.
[PERF] Max: tired, careful, almost formal. Marcus: level and unhurried. Two men who both know the other knows something, and neither will say what.
*(A stool scrapes. A cup is set down.)*
MAX: You eat?
MARCUS (Orlando): I ate.
MAX: That is new.
*(The soldering iron ticks once. Under it, the faint hiss in the coat.)*
MAX: That thing is running.
MARCUS (Orlando): It runs. I forget to stop it.
[PERF] True, as far as Marcus knows. He says it the way he says everything now: as a fact about his own head. Do not play it as a trick.
MAX: Mm.
[PLANT: the amnesia is Marcus's cover and his shield; Max cannot test it. Max chooses not to ask.]

## ACTION: the upgrade bench
MAX: Two things on the bench tonight. A damper for the cane's tip, so it taps soft on stone. Or a relay for the dog's collar, so she carries farther.
The player chooses one. Each confirms by sound: the damper with a soft rubber press and a muted tap; the relay with a brass snap and a faint chirp that Cookie's collar answers.
*(Marcus's hand drifts past the rack on his right, where the augment gain sits.)*
MAX: Gain's capped. Leave it.
*(Marcus's hand stops. It falls away. He says nothing.)*
[PERF] Marcus does not react at all. No breath, no pause, no tone. The silence is the line.
[PLANT: first listen is concern; second listen is the jailer's hand, and Marcus knows now whose hand it is.]
*(The key string on Max's chest clinks. Cookie lies down near the locked drawer, her collar settling. Marcus does not touch the drawer.)*
[PLANT: the drawer stays shut. Payoff: Act 3, the signet ring and Max's abandoned reels.]

## LINES: the decoy [SAFE]
MAX: Sit. I give you the where.
*(A stool. Max speaks slowly, in order, like a man reading.)*
MAX: East Dock. Aegis-Corvus keeps an annex on the seawall, past the cold stores. A records vault, old company files. If there is paper anywhere that can weaken her, it is there.
MARCUS (Orlando): Who told you?
MAX: A supplier.
[PERF] "Supplier" said like weather, the same as on the telephone in Scene 07. Do not warm it.
MAX: Night shift only. No patrol after midnight. A quiet man could walk in and out. No chamba tonight, only eyes. A look and leave.
[PLANT: a real target, a safe one; Tamsin will notice later that no guard was posted. Max's lead is told in order, with no breath in it, exactly like the second telling of the bridge story.]
MARCUS (Orlando): Say it again. East Dock.
MAX: East Dock. Aegis-Corvus keeps an annex on the seawall, past the cold stores. A records vault, old company files. If there is paper anywhere that can weaken her, it is there.
[PERF] Max repeats it word for word, not a syllable out of place. A man who forgets what day it is would be glad of the patience. A man who has listened for liars would not be.
[PLANT: second playthrough, the verbatim repeat is the first rehearsal tell.]
MARCUS (Orlando): That's a lot of detail for a supplier.
MAX: He is a talker.
*(A beat. The rain on the tin.)*
MAX: What is in the vault, I cannot swear to you. But there is a vault. That much is true.
[PLANT: the only wholly honest sentence in Max's lead. It is bait because it is true.]
*(The clock on the far wall strikes: a low cluster of twelve soft strokes, a rest, four rising, a rest, then seven high and eight lower. Max's hand stops on the bench. The soldering iron ticks into the silence.)*
[PLANT: 12/04/78 as a pitch pattern, Max's old unit sign. Do not explain.]
MARCUS (Orlando): That clock. Where'd you get it?
MAX: It came with the roof.
[PERF] Dry, a half beat quick. He wants the subject closed.

## ACTION: the door
Max crosses with him. Three bolts thrown back, one after another. The seal hisses. Cold rain and the first hum of the city.
MAX: East Dock. Trust me.
[PERF] Max: easy, finished, like a line he has been practising on the way over. Marcus does not hear the line as a line yet.
*(Max stops at the threshold. The eyepatch servo ticks twice. A very faint carrier hum begins, and the room seems to hold its breath.)*
MAX: Walk it in, walk it out, brother.
[PERF] The REHEARSED version. Clean. A quarter beat too fast. NO catch of breath after "in". NO knuckle tap on the frame on "out". "Brother" is warm, correct, and has no tiredness in it. Record the true version first for contrast, so the actor hears the difference.
*(There is no tap. The doorframe stays silent.)*
*(Cookie's collar ticks once. She turns her head toward Max. Marcus's own head tilts a degree. His hand finds the cane's grip and settles.)*
MARCUS (Orlando): That's a good lead, Max. Thank you. I mean that.
[PERF] TAKE A (the sincere take, recorded fully warm). Plain, a man thanking a friend. The listener should believe him completely.
[PERF] TAKE B (the held take, recorded after a breath). The same words, the warmth held a half beat too long, so that the sentence sounds finished and then continues. The game uses Take A on a first playthrough and Take B when the player has already caught the tell.
*(Max does not answer. A half beat of silence. The servo does not tick. The hum at the threshold continues.)*
[PLANT: Max suspects Marcus knows and lets it ride.]
MAX: *(low)* ...Vos. Eat something on the way.
[PERF] The one Spanish slip: tired, tender, caught. Do not repeat it.
MARCUS (Orlando): Walk it out.
[PERF] Exactly the way he has always said it. It costs him.
*(Max lets the door swing. Marcus steps through. The bolts go back, one, two, three, behind him. The hum fades with the door.)*

## SOUND: the alley
A drip from a broken pipe, LEFT. A two-beat gutter, RIGHT. The scanner hum overhead, thin but present. The workshop is two corners behind.
*(Marcus walks until the hum thickens under a Lantern Post and then thins again. He stops.)*
MARCUS (Orlando): *(to himself)* I can't tell you how he says it. I couldn't tell you what I ate yesterday.
[PERF] Not a boast. An inventory of the damage.

## PUZZLE: Real versus Rehearsed
Marcus takes the recorder from his coat. He rewinds. The player holds a button to play three takes from the reel, held close to his ear, low. Each take is the phrase "Walk it in, walk it out, brother." The three takes are labelled by the recorder's own click: one click, two clicks, three clicks.
- **Take one** (Visit 1, the workshop door): the true version. A breath catch after "in". A knuckle tap on "out". A weary "brother".
- **Take two** (Visit 2, the workshop door): the true version, again.
- **Take three** (tonight): clean, quarter beat too fast, no catch, no tap.
**The task:** pick the take that does not belong. The player presses to confirm. Taking a few listens is expected.
- **Hint 1:** Cookie shifts on take three: her collar ticks and she leans away from the recorder.
- **Hint 2:** MARCUS (Orlando): "Listen for the breath after 'in'."
- **Hint 3:** the reel loops the knuckle tap, takes one and two, then take three, so the missing tap is a hole in the loop.
**Failure feedback:** a wrong choice replays that take with the missing tell isolated: the breath, the tap, as clean marks that the odd take does not have. No buzzers, no timers, no fail state; the right choice always comes.
*(When the player chooses take three, the recorder clicks, and the reel keeps turning on its own for a beat, as if listening.)*
[PLANT: the proof is the recording, not Marcus's ear and not his memory. Marcus does not announce a gift. Cookie's shift is the first witness; the tape is the second.]
MARCUS (Orlando): ...There. There it is.
[PERF] Level, not triumphant. The sound of a man putting a name to a wrong note.
*(He rewinds, once. He plays the true take again. The tap rings. He does not play the clean one a second time.)*
MARCUS (Orlando): Same words. Same man. Just, it was wrong, girl. Like a song somebody learned off the radio.
*(Cookie's collar settles against his boot. She says nothing.)*
[PERF] Quiet. He is not angry yet. This is the part before anger, where a thing is simply too clear to argue with.
MARCUS (Orlando): He gave me East Dock. So I'm going to East Dock. And I'm going to look at everything on the way.
*(He thumbs the reel to stop. The hiss ends half a second late. Then the click.)*
[PLANT: the Madame listens; she will hear a man who has just learned something and said nothing about it.]
MARCUS (Orlando): I'm going to smile at him tomorrow, and I need you not to bite him.
[PERF] Gentle, almost a joke, with nothing under it that is funny. The line is a promise to himself, spoken to the dog.
*(Cookie exhales through her nose. Her collar jingles once. She leads.)*
**End of scene.** (Scene 14 follows: the Annex.)
