## SOUND: the market closing
Landmarks, all by ear. The off-pitch generator, LEFT and low. The frying stall, CENTRE, its hiss thinning to a tick. The wind chime of loose screws, FAR RIGHT, which marks the back alley and Roz's stall beyond it. Rain on awnings, drips into pots, the warped-jazz loop. Shutters rattle down in sequence. Pigeons shuffle under an awning, LEFT.
**MUSIC:** none.

## ACTION: the quiet walk
Marcus has the signal. Cookie leads him toward the way out. The player walks the length of the market with nothing to solve. The stalls shut around him.

MARCUS (Orlando): Four. Slow on the last.
*(A beat. He stops himself, then goes on. Her laugh fades.)*
KESSEL: Last hot bao! Last hot bao, nobody leaves hungry!
*(A paper bag is pressed into his hand, still warm.)*

*(A radio clicks off on the left. The jazz loop and the elder's evening voice are gone in the same half second. Dobbs, to nobody: "Yes, sir." The shutter drops.)*
[PERF] Marcus is light, almost easy. This is the last easy minute he will have. Do not play it as a farewell.

## SOUND: until dawn
**ACTION:** the player sits. A long held silence. Rain slows. A shutter creaks. Cookie lies down against his leg, and the collar settles. The generator is the only thing that never stops.
**MUSIC:** a single thin held tone, under almost nothing, for the last thirty seconds.

## SOUND: the six a.m. chime
Grey light is not for Marcus, but the air changes; the rain eases to a drip. He rises, lays Roz's shawl over her hands, and Cookie leads him out through the market gate into the courtyard where the morning patrol gathers for the sweep. Vance's sweep has begun; the guards arrive in pairs. Across the district, a bell strikes three bright hits and then waits in a muffled thud, like a bell with no clapper. The thud is felt more than heard.
In the gap, from every corner, flat voices answer in unison: STAFF: Settled.
**ACTION:** Marcus stands in a courtyard of silent patrols. Boots stop. Rifles stop swinging. Every guard goes still for exactly one second, the way a room goes still when a name is spoken.
*(A chime, a pause, the thud, "Settled," a second of breath held by hundreds of people.)*
Marcus stands in the middle of it, one hand on the cane. Cookie's collar jingles once. He does not tap the cane.
MARCUS (Orlando): *(almost to himself)* They all stopped.
[PERF] Quiet awe, a man who has just stood where no one should. [PLANT: the chime is the crest's bell struck with no clapper; the thud is where the fourth tone would sound; the conditioning at scale.]
Then the guards move again, as if nothing happened. Far off, a tram starts. The city exhales.
**Last page:** the sound of a city holding its breath, then the first morning bird, small and bright, a beat late.
