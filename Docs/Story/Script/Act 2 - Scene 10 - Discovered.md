# Act 2 - Scene 10 - Discovered

**Scene ID:** A2-S10 | **Act:** 2 | **Mission:** 2, sub-mission 3 (Discovered and the Reveal) | **Location:** the Cistern's flooded hall, the dais, the service hatch | **Status:** CONFIRMED by Orlando 2026-10-09 (Flanagan's draft with two cuts)
**Cast:** Marcus (Orlando) | Max (separate actor; Guatemalan, 60s, gravelly; one Spanish slip at most, "mira") | Cookie (separate actor; low, dry, close-mic; one word at most) | Teodora (Greek; calm, exact) | Ferro (Brazilian; slow, heavy) | Tamsin Okoro (Igbo-Cornish British; counts aloud) | Sol (Mexican, Oaxacan Zapotec; soft, hurt) | Wick (Vietnamese; young, fast, a whisper) | Pryce (Welsh; a precise, amused tenor, over a stolen radio) | the elder's voice (a faint, thin, old fragment).
**Run time played:** about 11 minutes. Every stretch has a player action. Nobody is hurt.

## SOUND: the hall
Landmarks, all by ear.
1. **The slow drip, AHEAD and below,** each drop returning almost two seconds late.
2. **Lamps, everywhere:** oil hiss, glass chimneys ticking.
3. **The dais and the iron door, FRONT RIGHT:** a cold metal hum, the door's latch ticking once in its frame.
4. **Boots on the catwalk, BEHIND and ringing in a half circle.**
The Lantern Post hum is absent. [PLANT: lead and water; the elder's eye is dead here.]
**MUSIC:** none. The limp is the score.

## ACTION: the search
**ACTION:** the player holds a button to keep Marcus's hand on Cookie's collar. Releasing it lets Cookie move, but she stays at his side. No combat is offered.
Teodora's hands pass over his coat: pockets unbuttoned, the concealed pockets found one by one. A flash device, a sonic emitter, a smoke capsule clink on a table.
TEODORA (calm, exact): Stand still. I will not ask twice.
MARCUS (Orlando): I'm standing.
*(She lifts the cane from his hand. It collapses in her hold: a ratchet of nested segments, a snap, a lock. A short katana, straight and light, with a low singing note. She does not hide it. She lets him hear it in plain sight.)*
[PERF] Marcus does not move. He does not reach. He is a man standing in a room full of people who could take him apart, and he is holding the one thing that matters: the dog.
*(Cookie's growl builds. Marcus's grip on her collar tightens.)*
MARCUS (Orlando): Easy, Cook.
*(The growl drops to a rumble and holds.)*
FERRO (slow, heavy): You think a cane makes you harmless? It makes you careless.
MARCUS (Orlando): I think a cane makes me a man you can see coming. You didn't see me coming.
[PERF] Level, quiet, not a boast. The anger is in the control.
TAMSIN (counting): Eight darts. Eight spent. Not one in him.
SOL (small, hurt): I opened the hatch. I let him in.
WICK (whisper, to someone beside him): It's the Echo. It's really him.
TEODORA (calm, exact): Who sent you?
MARCUS (Orlando): Nobody.
TEODORA: Nobody does not know the knock.
MARCUS (Orlando): A woman named Roz Adeyemi taught it to me. She's dead.
*(Tamsin's counting stops. The lamps hiss.)*
TAMSIN (quietly): I know that name.
[PLANT: Tamsin sees him clearly before anyone else does.]
TEODORA: He should see the Keeper.
MARCUS (Orlando): The Keeper.
TEODORA: He speaks only inside.
[PLANT: the rota and slate from Scene 09; "inside" is the lead walls.]

## ACTION: the iron door
The player walks with Cookie toward the dais; she leads, but at the iron door she stops, the collar settling, and does not growl. [PLANT: Max's scent is old to her; she knows it.]
The latch ticks. The iron door scrapes open.
*(The hall goes quiet. The lamps still hiss, but every voice stops. A chair scrapes.)*
*(Then a step. A drag. A step.)*
*(Across the stone floor, an uneven, patient walk. A servo whirs softly. A key on a string clinks against a chest.)*
*(Marcus's head turns by a degree. His breathing slows. His hand stays on the collar.)*
[PERF] Do not play surprise. Play recognition arriving slowly, a body understanding before the mind agrees.
MARCUS (Orlando): *(very quiet)* Max.
*(The limp stops. Mid-drag. A silence the size of the hall. The drip returns, almost two seconds late.)*
[PLANT: the limp is the score; the stop is the scene.]

## LINES: the reveal [SAFE]
MAX: *(low, rough)* Sit down, Marcus. Please.
[PERF] It is the first time Max has ever said "please". It should sound like a word he has to look for.
*(Nobody breathes. Wick, in a whisper: "He said please.")*
MAX: *(after a beat)* ...Hell. Maybe I wanted you to.
[PERF] A confession of a smaller thing, carried on the larger one. He is almost relieved.
MARCUS (Orlando): Wanted me to what?
MAX: Find the lamps.
*(Cookie's collar settles slowly, a quiet jingle. She does not growl. A breath.)*
COOKIE (low, dry, close): Marcus.
*(Marcus does not answer her. The rain through the vents is the only sound.)*
MARCUS (Orlando): How long?
MAX: Since the lamps. Before you came to my bench.
MARCUS (Orlando): You sat in that workshop and poured my coffee.
MAX: I did.
MARCUS (Orlando): I told you about the hole where Tuesday was. You handed me a screwdriver.
[PERF] Quiet. Each sentence lands on its own, with the anger held under it like water behind a door.
MAX: *(a breath)* I did that too.

## LINES: the partial truth [SAFE]
MAX: The lead was mine. The tubes. The ferry. I gave it to you in the workshop weeks ago, and you wrote it on a tape and forgot where it came from.
MARCUS (Orlando): So I walked through your door by your own hand.
MAX: You walked through it because you listen. I only left it unlocked.
MARCUS (Orlando): Why not tell me?
MAX: Because the walls here are the only place anyone can talk. The city listens. Here, nothing does. And because you would have gone after her alone, and I would have been picking you up off her stairs.
[PERF] "Her" is not named. He says it the way he has said it for years: like a weather.
MARCUS (Orlando): You don't get to decide that for me.
MAX: *(very low)* I know. Mira, I would have told you.
[PERF] The one Spanish slip. Tired, tender, caught. Do not repeat it.
[PLANT: Max never mentions Marcus's father, the elder, or any sister. He tells the truth he can carry and no more.]
MARCUS (Orlando): What else?
MAX: Nothing that helps you tonight.
MARCUS (Orlando): That's not a no.
MAX: It's not a yes.
[PLANT: Marcus notices the gap and does not push; the amnesia keeps him from knowing what to push at.]

## ACTION: the cane
MAX: Give it back to him. He knows how to use it, and he knows how not to.
*(A pause. Teodora's boots shift. The short katana folds with a ratchet and a snap, and the cane is pressed into his hand. His fingers close.)*
[PLANT: the lethal cane is returned by the man who built it; Marcus's refusals to kill carry the weight.]
TEODORA: Keeper.
MAX: Stand down, Teodora.
TEODORA: Standing down.
*(She steps back.)*
MAX: Stay. Rest. One night. The bunks are dry.
MARCUS (Orlando): Not here.
MAX: Then hear me. There is one more place I need you to look.
MARCUS (Orlando): Where?
MAX: I will give you the where in the workshop, where the walls are mine. Tomorrow.
MARCUS (Orlando): Tomorrow.
[PERF] Not agreement. A man filing a debt he intends to collect.

## SOUND and LINES: the cliffhanger
**ACTION:** the player walks with Cookie toward the hatch. Max walks behind them: step, drag, step, but the rhythm has changed, a half beat slower.
*(In the hall, behind a stack of crates, a stolen radio crackles. Static. Then a precise, amused Welsh tenor.)*
PRYCE (over the radio): Mr. Cross is a guest of the resistance. Isn't that charming.
*(Silence. Every boot in the hall stops. The lamps hiss.)*
TAMSIN (counting, thin): That set was off. It was off.
TEODORA: Wick, find the set.
MARCUS (Orlando): How does he know I'm here?
MAX: *(too fast)* He does not. Go.
[PERF] Quiet, quick. A lie told by a man who knows what a lie sounds like.
[PLANT: Aegis knows exactly where they are; the elder lets the rebellion live. Do not explain.]

## ACTION: the hatch
At the service hatch, lead walls and water behind them, outside lies the culvert and the night.
MAX: *(low, rough, with a catch of breath after "in")* Walk it in, walk it out, brother.
*(A knuckle taps on the frame on "out".)*
[PERF] The TRUE version, said tired and not smooth. He is trying to repair something with the only thing he has.
*(Marcus does not answer. A first silence between them. The drip. The tap of Max's knuckle still ringing on the frame.)*
*(Marcus steps through. At the threshold a faint carrier hum begins, and Max's eyepatch servo ticks twice. Under the hum, thin and very old, at the edge of hearing, a single word:)*
ELDER (faint, thin, old): ...asleep...
MAX: *(a half beat late)* Mind the step.
[AUDIO-ONLY] Marcus catches the word and says nothing. The cane taps the stone, once.

### [AUDIO-ONLY] Second-playthrough layer
On a second playthrough, with the hum louder, the full transmission is audible:
ELDER (thin, old, measured; a faint wet click at the end of the sentence): Is he asleep? Good. Keep him tired. Sleep is when the boy is most himself.
[PERF] Slow, courteous, almost kind. Never raise the voice. The name is never spoken.
[PLANT: the elder listens through Max's eye; the workshop and the Cistern are the only places he cannot.]
**Sub-mission 3 ends. Mission 2 ends.** (Scene 11 follows.)
