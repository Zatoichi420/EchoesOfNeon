# Act 2 - Scene 11 - Hollis and the Pill Tin

**Scene ID:** A2-S11 | **Act:** 2 | **Mission:** Between Mission 2 and Mission 3, the morning after the reveal | **Location:** Foundry Belt streets, a room above a tea stall, then the lab at night | **Status:** CONFIRMED by Orlando 2026-10-09 (Flanagan's draft, no changes)
**Cast:** Marcus (Orlando) | Hollis Barre (Ghanaian, Akan English; 70, retired tram conductor; dry, courtly, gentle; marker "ei") | Kit (Filipino English; 12; bright, bored; counts aloud; marker "po"; creole word "sulit") | Mrs. Perera (tea stall owner; Sri Lankan Tamil English; background voice; marker "aiyo") | Desi (separate actor; a recording, dry and close, no reverb) | Cookie (separate actor; no spoken lines here) | Marcus's smart glasses (flat voice).
**Run time played:** about 11 minutes. Every stretch has a player action.

## SOUND: the street and the stairs
Landmarks, all by ear.
1. **A kettle shrieking at the tea stall, LEFT and below,** cups clinking, a ladle on tin.
2. **The tram line overhead:** a clatter of rail seams every twenty seconds, a bell, then a long hum fading.
3. **A radiator ticking, AHEAD, through the open door above.**
Early morning. Wet stone, a pigeon shuffling on a ledge, a delivery cart. The Lantern Post hum is thin here.
**MUSIC:** none.

## ACTION: the walk
Marcus has not slept. Cookie leads, but Kit meets them at the tram stop, a tray of batteries clinking at her hip.
KIT: One, two, three, four. *(clap)* Cane man. You look like a wet coat.
MARCUS (Orlando): Long night.
KIT: Two coins and I'll take you up. It's sulit, po.
MARCUS (Orlando): Where?
KIT: Mr. Hollis. He says the Doctor's husband always comes on the third day he doesn't eat.
MARCUS (Orlando): I eat.
KIT: Sure.
[PERF] Flat, tired, almost smiling. He says nothing about the night. Do not play anger; play a man with no room left for it.
*(He pays her. Coins ring in her palm. Kit counts the stairs aloud as they climb.)*
KIT: One, two, three. Three is loose, po.
*(The third stair gives under his foot, a soft creak. Cookie steps over it.)*
MRS. PERERA (from below): Aiyo, you will wake the whole house, child.

## LINES: the door [SAFE]
HOLLIS: Door's open. Mind the third stair.
MARCUS (Orlando): Mr. Barre?
HOLLIS: Say that again.
MARCUS (Orlando): Mr. Barre. I'm Marcus.
*(A long pause. A chair scrapes. The radiator ticks.)*
HOLLIS: Doctor Cross's man. You talk to the dog like she's a colleague.
MARCUS (Orlando): She told you that?
HOLLIS: She told me everything. She was a terrible gossip about you.
[PERF] Hollis is warm and does not push. He lets the silence fall where it falls.
*(Marcus almost laughs. It does not quite arrive.)*
HOLLIS: Ei. You have not slept. A man who has not slept walks like a clerk.
MARCUS (Orlando): I'll sit.
HOLLIS: Sit. There is tea. It is not good. It is hot.
[PLANT: the echo of Max's "It is not good coffee. It is hot." Do not underline it.]

## LINES: the pill tin [SAFE]
A kettle pours. A cup is set down. Then a brass tin is pressed into his hand, warm from a pocket.
HOLLIS: She filled this every month. Seven pills. Never six, never eight. She said seven is lucky because it is uneven. Nothing divides it.
*(Marcus's thumb finds the lid. His glasses read the engraving in their flat voice.)*
GLASSES: Lid. Engraved. H. Barre. Seven. Not with coffee.
MARCUS (Orlando): She pressed hard. Right hand, she always had a heavy right hand with a pen.
HOLLIS: Left, son. Left. She smeared everything with her wrist.
MARCUS (Orlando): ...Left. Yes.
[PERF] A half beat. He accepts the correction like a man taking a coin he doesn't remember dropping. [PLANT: the Inventory drift begins; do not underline.]
*(Cookie shifts against his leg.)*
HOLLIS: I never took the last one. Couldn't.
MARCUS (Orlando): Keep it.
HOLLIS: I am giving it to you, son. She would have wanted it with the person who tuned the dog.

## LINES: Desi, told by someone else [SAFE]
HOLLIS: She wore a green scarf with a hole in it. She said it was the only warm thing she owned that had never let her down. She scolded me for the stairs. "Hollis Barre, you climb those seventy times a week, you are not a pigeon."
*(A small laugh in the old man's throat.)*
HOLLIS: And she laughed in two pieces. A small one first, like she was checking the room was safe. Then the big one.
MARCUS (Orlando): *(very quiet)* Yeah.
[PERF] He says it like a man who has been given back a piece of something he thought was lost. Let the pause after it hold.
HOLLIS: She said you could hear a lie in a pause. She said she married a lie detector and never won an argument.
MARCUS (Orlando): I lose arguments about what day it is.
[PERF] Dry, almost a joke, with nothing behind it but an admission. Minimal humor: the beat is tender, not funny.
HOLLIS: *(gently)* She said that too.

## PUZZLE: The Bent Tooth
Hollis fetches a small wooden music box from the shelf. A crank, a comb, a brass cylinder.
HOLLIS: She mended this for me. One tooth was bent. She said she would fix it next month.
*(He winds it. A thin, bright tune begins and stumbles on the fourth note, a clinking thud where a pitch should fall.)*
MARCUS (Orlando): That's not how it goes.
The player turns the cylinder with a left or right stereo cue: a small click for each pin that shifts earlier or later against the comb. Listen for the fourth note to drop into place.
- **Hint 1:** Cookie's ear tilts, her collar tinkles, and she whines near the correct pitch.
- **Hint 2:** MARCUS (Orlando): "The fourth note drops."
- **Hint 3:** a faint dream hum of Desi plays under the box, the fourth note landing right.
No buzzers. No timers. No fail state: a wrong turn only makes the tune stumble again.
*(When the fourth note drops, the tune runs clean to the end. A latch clicks in the box's base.)*
[PLANT: the tune is Desi's; Marcus knows it is wrong before anyone says so.]

## SOUND and LINES: the recording [SAFE]
A small door springs open under the box. A tiny spool turns. A dry, close voice, tired and warm. No reverb.
DESI (recorded): One. Two. Three. *(a pill ticks in a tin)* Four. Five. Six. Seven. Never six. Never eight. Seven.
[PERF] Counting, absorbed, like a woman who has done this a hundred times and still likes it.
DESI (recorded): Mr. Barre will say he can't tell the blue from the white. It's the white one. The one that tastes like chalk.
DESI (recorded): Marcus says I count out loud when I'm worried. I am not worried. I am counting.
*(She hums under her breath. On the sixth note it slips a half step flat.)*
[PLANT: the lying note, again.]
DESI (recorded): There. Lid on.
DESI (recorded): If you're hearing this, you found the box. So you're sitting with someone. Good. Do not sit alone with a tin.
DESI (recorded): Eat something, love. A real meal. And be kind to the dog. She counts too.
*(A small click. The spool stops.)*
*(Nobody speaks. The radiator ticks. Kit, at the door, does not clap.)*
MARCUS (Orlando): *(quietly)* She counted when she was worried.
[PERF] Not a revelation. A fact put back on the shelf where it belongs.

## CLUE: the notice
A paper rustles on the door frame, pinned with a tack. Kit reads it aloud.
KIT: "Notice of Clearance. Tram Row, Block Fourteen. Residents will vacate within nine days. Aegis-Corvus Housing Compliance thanks you for your cooperation." *(a shrug in the voice)* They say that every year, po.
HOLLIS: They said it the year the foundry burned. Nobody believed it either.
*(Marcus's hand tightens on the cane. A small, slow breath. He says nothing.)*
[PLANT: Marcus's first act in the Chair spares this building; do not resolve here. The silent reaction is the whole beat.]

HOLLIS: The clinic had a back door. She called it the doctor's door. I came with a cough and a pride, two days after the tram line cut my hours. She took no money. She said, "Bring tea." I have brought tea ever since.

HOLLIS: Off my old conductor's staff. For the cane. It will ring warmer.
*(Marcus fits it to the cane's tip. The next tap on the floor rings brighter, warmer, longer.)*

## ACTION: the second refusal
**Cut to the lab at night.** Rain on the window. The rack's hum. The reel's idle. The recorder is in front of him.
**ACTION:** the player presses record. Marcus's hand lifts off the key before the hiss starts.
MARCUS (Orlando): Not tonight.
[PERF] Quiet. A statement, not a failure. He is not unable; he is choosing. This is the second refusal.
*(Cookie goes still. Her collar stops. The reel idles. No hiss begins. Nothing ends half a second late, because nothing began.)*
[PLANT: the second refusal: a tape not made; the Madame listens; the third is the unfinished tap in the Chair.]
**End of scene.** (Scene 12 follows.)
