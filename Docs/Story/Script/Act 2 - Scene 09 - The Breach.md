# Act 2 - Scene 09 - The Breach

**Scene ID:** A2-S09 | **Act:** 2 | **Mission:** 2, sub-mission 2 (The Breach) | **Location:** the Cistern's steel door, a service hatch, the pipe gallery, a drained culvert, the flooded hall | **Status:** CONFIRMED by Orlando 2026-10-09 (Bernardin's draft with three continuity fixes)
**Cast:** Marcus (Orlando) | Cookie (separate actor; low, dry, close-mic; one short line) | Sol (Mexican, Oaxacan Zapotec background; soft, hesitant; signal tech) | Tamsin Okoro (Igbo-Cornish British; ex-Aegis dispatcher; counts aloud) | Teodora (Greek; ex-Aegis nurse; calm, exact) | Ferro (Brazilian; slow, heavy) | Wick (Vietnamese; young, fast, high) | Marcus's smart glasses (flat voice).
**Run time played:** about 10 minutes. Every stretch has a player action. Nobody is hurt: the resistance wants Marcus alive.

## SOUND: the Cistern, by ear
Landmarks, all by ear.
1. **A slow drip, AHEAD and below:** one drop every few seconds, each return almost two seconds late. A huge flooded hall.
2. **Lamps, everywhere:** the hiss of oil, glass chimneys ticking as they cool, a warm lamp smell drifting from the vents.
3. **Paper and chalk:** a rustle, a slate, a chalk scratch, and taps along pipe rails. Plans move on paper and pipe, never by radio.
4. **The pump drone,** now thick behind walls.
The Lantern Post hum is gone. [PLANT: lead-lined walls and water; the elder's eye is dead here.]
**Do not pulse:** a sonar pulse rings the whole hall like a bell, and every pipe rail answers. The player uses cane taps and Cookie's rustle instead.
**MUSIC:** none.

## PUZZLE: The Knock
The previous scene left him at the door with "Two." The player now knocks the rest and the three.
- **Rushed rest:** the steel plays the knock back a half tone warm. Retry.
- **Hint 1:** from the far side, a soft, slow answer: two, rest, three.
- **Hint 2:** Cookie taps a paw twice, waits, taps three times.
- **Hint 3:** MARCUS (Orlando): "Two. Wait. Three. Wait longer than you think."
**No buzzers, no fail state.** A good rest does not open the front door. A small bolt slides low and left, and a hinge squeals: a service hatch.

## LINES: the hatch [SAFE]
VOICE (through the hatch, muffled): Not since the flood.
SOL (soft, hesitant): Ayo? You are late. Please, hurry, it is wet down there.
MARCUS (Orlando): I'm here.
[PERF] True and nothing more. He does not claim the name. He keeps his mouth shut and lets the mistake do the work.
SOL: Please don't make a noise in here. Everything rings. You can hear it too, can't you? The hum under the water.
MARCUS (Orlando): Understood.
SOL: The pipe gallery, left. Follow the dripping that counts. The wet part is easy chamba.
*(The hatch seals. A switch ticks. Sol's step retreats to a panel.)*
[PLANT: Marcus is mistaken for a courier named Ayo; the hall's trust is built on a late man.]

## PUZZLE: The Pipe Gallery
Dozens of pipes along a low ceiling, each dripping at its own pace. One counts: four drips, the fourth slow, then silence.
- **Hint 1:** Cookie's jingle follows the counting pipe and drops to a rustle at the others.
- **Hint 2:** MARCUS (Orlando): "That one counts."
- **Hint 3:** the true pipe's drip rises a half step.
**No fail state.** A wrong pipe dead-ends at a valve; the drips go dull and Cookie waits.
*(At the counting pipe his cane taps the rail once, slow. He does not notice.)*
[PLANT: the closet rhyme, told in water.]

## ACTION: the culvert
A drained culvert. Dry concrete rings under every step; strips of sludge muffle it. Two figures wait ahead with a dart tube.
WICK (young, fast, high): Ayo's late. If he's not through by the chime, I'm telling Teodora.
FERRO (slow, heavy): Ayo is never late. Ayo is a clock.
- **Hint 1:** Cookie's jingle goes quiet on sludge and returns on concrete.
- **Hint 2:** MARCUS (Orlando): "Soft ground."
- **Hint 3:** Cookie leads along the sludge strips and stops behind a pillar.
A wrong step brings a whisper-pop, a tick of metal and a soft thud. A dart means the beat replays from the start of the culvert. Nothing is lost.
*(The player passes. A dart rings on a pipe a hand's breadth from his shoulder, a warning that costs nothing.)*
MARCUS (Orlando): *(under his breath, to Cookie)* They want me breathing. Good. So do I.
[PERF] Dry, quick, a man noting the house rules. Minimal humor: this is relief, not a joke.
FERRO (slow, heavy): Hold the next pipe. If he is a patrol, we hear his boots.
WICK (young, fast, high): Boots, yes. I hear no boots. I hear a dog.
FERRO: Dogs are not couriers.
WICK: Ayo has been strange lately.
*(Their argument fades behind a pillar as the player slips past on sludge.)*
[PLANT: the lookouts are future allies; they hesitate; they do not fire to kill.]

## SOUND and CLUE: the hall
A stair rises to a catwalk above the flooded floor. Hundreds of lamps hiss at once.
GLASSES (flat): Rota. Hand-lettered. Watch one, Teodora. Watch two, Ferro. Watch three, Wick. Courier, Ayo, due at the chime.
MARCUS (Orlando): Ayo's the courier.
GLASSES: Map. A pin through the Foundry Belt. A second pin, circled, in Corvus Heights.
GLASSES: Slate. Chalk. "The Keeper speaks only inside."
MARCUS (Orlando): Only inside.
[PERF] A man filing a rule. [PLANT: "the Keeper" is a title; nobody here says who it is.]
At a desk of logs, TAMSIN counts aloud, a dispatcher's cadence, without meaning to.
TAMSIN (counting): Lamp one hundred and four. One hundred and five. Spill reported, four thousand litres. Measured, nil. Nil. Nil.
GLASSES: Log. Spill reported, four thousand litres. Measured, zero.
MARCUS (Orlando): Four thousand litres of nothing.
*(Near an iron door by the dais, Cookie stops dead. No growl, no whine. The jingle comes back slow.)*
MARCUS (Orlando): Cook?
*(She says nothing.)*
[PLANT: Cookie knows a smell and never says so.]
