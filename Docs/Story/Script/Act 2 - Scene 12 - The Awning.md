# Act 2 - Scene 12 - The Awning

**Scene ID:** A2-S12 | **Act:** 2 | **Mission:** Between Mission 2 and Mission 3 | **Location:** a tram stop awning on the road to Max's workshop, in the rain | **Status:** CONFIRMED by Orlando 2026-10-09 (Smith's draft with two changes)
**Cast:** Marcus (Orlando) | G (separate actor; low, calm, close, no accent, no breath behind the voice) | Cookie (separate actor; low, dry, close-mic; one short line only) | a tram announcer (Arabic, then English; flat, falling note) | a passer-by (Ghanaian English; marker "chale").
**Run time played:** about 6 minutes. Every stretch has a player action. Nobody is hurt.

## SOUND: the tram stop
Landmarks, all by ear.
1. **Rain on a tin awning, overhead,** loud and steady, with a drip into a bucket, LEFT.
2. **A kiosk radiator, RIGHT,** a steady dry tick.
3. **A loose iron step at the platform edge, AHEAD,** a soft creak when anything shifts.
4. **The tram line, far:** rail seams clattering, a bell, a long hum fading.
Far above, a billboard strikes three bright bell hits and waits. [PLANT: the fourth tone withheld.]
**MUSIC:** none.

## ACTION: waiting
Marcus is on his way to Max's workshop, the "tomorrow" Max promised. The player stands under the awning. A bench is behind him. Cookie sits at his feet; the collar settles.
*(A passer-by, quick wet footsteps.)*
PASSER-BY: Chale, this rain! Keep dry, my friend.
MARCUS (Orlando): Thanks.
*(She hurries on. The tram announcer, far down the line, speaks in Arabic, then English, ending on the same falling note.)*
ANNOUNCER: Next arrival, ten past. Aegis-Corvus thanks you for your patience.
MARCUS (Orlando): What time is the tram?
*(Nobody is there to answer. He sits. The bench takes his weight.)*

## PUZZLE: the quiet seat
The rain on the awning goes thin, for one beat, on the left side of the bench. No footsteps yet.
**ACTION:** the player listens. Then the steps come, loud, even, deliberate, on wet stone, a man walking so as to be heard.
- **Hint 1:** Cookie's collar stops and she turns her head left. A rustle.
- **Hint 2:** MARCUS (Orlando): "The rain's thin over there."
- **Hint 3:** the player presses pulse: the awning, the radiator, the step, and a gap in the return a few metres left, a man's height of nothing, at the bench's far end. A beat later, a man's weight settles on the bench with a creak. The hole is gone.
**No fail state:** the steps arrive either way.
[PLANT: the thinning rain and the sonar hole are two of G's uncanny tells; Marcus never names them.]

## LINES: the bench [SAFE]
*(A coat shifts. A belt buckle ticks. A cuff. Two hands settling one over the other. No breath.)*
G: Is this seat taken?
MARCUS (Orlando): It's a bench.
G: Thank you.
[PERF] G: even, polite, unhurried. No menace in the pitch, and no warmth either. A man reading a card.
*(Marcus's head tilts a degree. His ear has caught something and cannot place it.)*
MARCUS (Orlando): Your voice.
G: It's an ordinary voice.
MARCUS (Orlando): Yes. That's what I can't place.
[PLANT: Marcus half-recognizes the voice from the market ("Done."), but his ear finds only an ordinary voice and a missing breath; the amnesia and the sonar hole keep him from placing it. He never names it.]
*(The radiator's tick slows. Tick. A long gap. Tick.)*
G: Rain's bad.
MARCUS (Orlando): It's been worse.
G: Yes.
*(The rain on the tin goes on. The silence between lines stretches a half beat too long.)*
*(Cookie's collar stops. She makes no sound. Then, from the bench, a low throat hum, three or four notes, almost under hearing. Cookie answers once, a single low note, and stops.)*
[PLANT: G's low held hum and Cookie's answer; the attack-night trigger, Act 3. Marcus misreads it as the radiator.]
MARCUS (Orlando): She doesn't usually do that.
G: Dog's good. Someone trained her well.
MARCUS (Orlando): My wife.
G: Sorry.
[PERF] The apology is exactly one word. Do not soften it and do not hurry it. Let the rain have the pause after.

## LINES: the questions [SAFE]
MARCUS (Orlando): What time did you say the tram was?
G: Ten past.
MARCUS (Orlando): You didn't say it.
G: No. The announcer did.
MARCUS (Orlando): ...Right.
*(A pause. The radiator ticks. Marcus's head tilts a degree.)*
MARCUS (Orlando): What time is the tram?
G: Ten past. It's all right.
[PERF] G: the same warmth as the first time, which is no warmth. The kindness is the unease. He does not point out that Marcus has asked before.
[PLANT: the amnesia leak; G is courteous to a man who has forgotten asking. He knows exactly what it means and says nothing.]
MARCUS (Orlando): Long day.
G: Yes.
MARCUS (Orlando): Are you with Aegis?
*(A beat. The rain thins again, then recovers.)*
G: Not tonight.
MARCUS (Orlando): That's an odd way to answer.
G: It's an odd night.
[PLANT: "Not tonight." reflects the Madame's off-scene order, "Don't touch him. Let him finish."]
*(Marcus's hand moves to the cane at his knee. The cane begins to collapse: a ratchet of segments.)*
COOKIE (low, dry, close): Marcus. Don't.
*(He stops. The segments click back. His hand settles on the grip.)*
[PERF] Cookie: low, flat, not afraid. A colleague's warning. Marcus does not question her; he just obeys her.
MARCUS (Orlando): You walk like you want me to hear you.
G: Yes. I thought it was polite.
[PLANT: G's steps are audible only here, on purpose, so he can be found. After tonight they vanish.]
*(His hands do not touch the cane. They stay folded, one over the other. Cuff on cuff.)*

## LINES: the glasses [OPTIONAL PLANT]
*(A tram bell, far off. Marcus turns his face toward the sound.)*
MARCUS (Orlando): I keep her glasses on the nightstand. Don't ask me why. I can't see them.
*(A very small silence. The rain thins and returns.)*
G: Glasses.
[PERF] G says it once, flat, a half step lower. He does not say "I'm sorry" again. Marcus does not remark on it.
[PLANT: G placed Desi's glasses on the nightstand after the attack. Do not underline.]

## ACTION: the step
The tram sounds nearer. The bell rings; the rail seams clatter.
**ACTION:** the player rises. The platform is ahead.
G: Mind the step. It's loose.
*(The iron creaks under Cookie's paw. Marcus steps around it.)*
MARCUS (Orlando): You noticed that from sitting?
G: I notice things. I don't like it when people fall.
[PERF] Plain, a man mentioning the weather. The line must not sound like a threat or a promise.
*(The tram arrives: a hiss of brakes, the doors sighing. The announcer, flat and falling: "Foundry Belt.")*
*(The bench creaks as G rises. His steps are loud on the wet stone: one, two, three.)*
G: Goodnight, Mr. Cross.
*(The steps stop. Marcus has not moved.)*
MARCUS (Orlando): I didn't give you my name.
G: No.
*(The tram bell rings, twice, and the doors close. The rain on the awning goes thin for one last beat. Then his steps leave, and as they leave they get quieter than they should, and then are not there at all.)*
[PERF] Marcus: level, not frightened. A man collecting a fact he cannot place.

## SOUND and ACTION: after
The tram pulls away. The rain on the awning returns to full. The radiator ticks at its normal pace.
**ACTION:** the player may press pulse. It returns the awning, the step, the bench, and nothing else.
MARCUS (Orlando): Cook. Who was that?
*(Cookie's collar settles. She does not answer.)*
MARCUS (Orlando): Quiet man, for a loud walker.
*(Far above, the billboard strikes three bright bell hits and waits. Marcus does not tap the cane. He turns toward the workshop. Cookie leads.)*
**End of scene.** (Scene 13 follows.)
